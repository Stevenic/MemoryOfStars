# planning/ — the spoiler layer

> **⚠ TOTAL SPOILERS AHEAD.** This folder holds the series' endings — the meta-mystery,
> the 30-book roadmap, and the tech underneath the wonder. **Readers: turn back.**
> It is committed to the public repo by author decision (2026-08) so contributors can
> work with the full picture.

## What lives here
- **`the-long-mystery.md`** — the meta-mystery (the Erasure) + the two-tier arc, the
  cycle charters, the craft-device schedule, and the full 30-book roadmap.
- **`tech-bible.md`** — the real mechanisms behind the series' wonders (never stated on
  the page).
- **`science-and-concepts.md`** — concept work and seams.
- Concept work and redesigns for unwritten books.

## The one rule that survives the un-gitignoring

**This layer never leaks into the reader-facing surfaces.** Nothing from planning/ may
appear in `series-bible/`, `manuscripts/`, or the website (`docs/`) beyond what a
*published book has itself revealed*. The website's Archive keeps its dark, unnamed
stars; the books keep their mysteries; the reader who avoids this folder gets the
series as intended. A curious reader who opens it has spoiled only themself.

Practical corollaries for contributors:
- The `write` (drafting) pass must not consult this layer — devices live in outlining
  and critique, never in the drafter's hands (see the operating rule in
  `the-long-mystery.md`).
- Book-level `canon-ledger.md` files log only what their own book establishes.
- Unwritten-book endings are discussed here and nowhere else.
