## 0. Discipline (hold this)
- **Hard SF: the universe obeys known physics.** No FTL *matter*, no violating relativity. Wormholes are GR-permitted (exotic matter is the honest caveat). "Instant comms" is treated honestly as a *channel to the past* (FTL signalling = time travel), governed by self-consistency (Novikov).
- **Texture, not lecture.** Physics is the *vocabulary of wonder and plausibility*, never the load-bearing explanation. Show a Dyson shell; never explain who built it or how it's powered. The deepest mechanics stay **undefined** (see the intentionally-undefined list).
- **Author's provenance:** the two cores (temporal mesh + telepresence) are the author's, conceived 1985 at age 16.

## 1. The keystone
**A "memory" is a *volumetric recording of an experience*, stored in the mesh, that can be
RE-ENTERED (walked through) — not merely recalled.** This is the atom the whole system runs
on, and *what the Erasure garbage-collects.*

## 2. The core primitive — temporal micro-wormholes
- Carry **information, not matter.** Once created, a wormhole is a channel that can send data back in time — **only as far as its own creation** (real physics: Morris–Thorne–Yurtsever).
- **Created in pairs** (entanglement-*flavored*); one is **physically ferried (sublight) to the destination** → a **beacon.** One transmitter is one-directional; bidirectional = a pair.
- **Self-consistency (Novikov):** the channel can only carry what was always going to be true. **You can hear, but not change.**
- **Exotic matter is scarce** ⇒ transmitters are precious; **cannot be made** by the current age, only **moved.** Total count only **decreases** (a finite, dwindling inheritance).

## 3. The system layers (all one inherited system)
- **Temporal Transmitter → the mesh.** Beacons form a routed, multi-hop graph (~10–20 per node). **Range = age**; a route's reach is capped by its *newest* beacon (oldest = deepest, most fought-over). **Reconfiguration:** can't make, only *move* — civilizations are surgeons of the mesh; cutting a rival's beacon erases them from the conversation (human-scale mirror of the Erasure).
- **Temporal Processor.** A wormhole looped in *time* locally → "instant" computation, but **self-consistent problems only** (Deutsch/Aaronson: CTCs ⇒ PSPACE). Paradox-problems have no solution → the machine hangs/fails/destroys itself. Yields **bootstrap/orphan knowledge** (answers with no origin — maybe the network itself).
- **Telepresence.** Two modes on the same tech:
  - **Real-time** — inhabit a *live* body (android, or a person) across the mesh. Presence, not travel.
  - **Simulation mode** — inhabit a *recorded memory* (a volumetric recording): free to explore, not to change (the fixed past = self-consistency again).
- **Reality capture ("portal, not screen").** Ambient volumetric scanning; the near-future *root* of the whole tree. This is *what makes memories* (and what fills storage).

## 4. The gradient (us → the ancients) — the reader's on-ramp
1. **Near-future:** local volumetric telepresence + ambient capture; screens obsolesce.
2. **Mid:** android inhabitation + interstellar telepresence over the mesh.
3. **Ancient/mythic:** the **Gate** — telepresence across space, *time, and death.*
→ *"portal to the game"* and *"portal to another life"* are the **same technology at different scales.**

## 5. Canon retro-fits (Books 1–2, consistent — do not contradict the text)
- **The Gate** = an ancient, extreme telepresence/inhabitation node. **The Pilgrimage** = that system decayed into ritual/myth. (Lyra "fell forward into another life" = inhabitation.)
- **The anchors** (Sael built; "no longer remembered what they held") = ancient temporal-mesh **beacons.**
- **The House of Memories** = a place running **sim-mode** (Lyra piloted Mara's recorded memory).
- **The Resonance Lens** (Book 2) = a **sim-mode viewer** — walk the recorded memories in an object ("objects remember people" = objects as capture points). The **Hall of Names** = an archive/index node of the mesh.
- **Book 1's uncanny "knowing"** (the woman in blue; the *seventh* bell; "someone should have been there") = **temporal leakage / future observers** bleeding into the past (the "crowded past").
- **Seren** (never sleeps, appears/vanishes, "several different men") = **distributed / telepresent** presence.
- **"Cities losing sequence"** = **consistency collapse** — GC deleting a load-bearing *looped* memory unstitches local cause & effect.

## 6. Constraints that keep it from breaking the universe
- **Matter still can't move.** Telepresence = *presence without reach*: you can visit a far world through a body but never *go*, never bring anything back. Preserves Law 1's loneliness & Law 7's wonder.
- **Mesh-only + bandwidth-limited + latency-sensitive** ⇒ interstellar presence is precious; the frontier is dark.
- **Beacon-transport is sublight** ⇒ new links need slow voyages; the mesh is *a map of every journey ever made.* (This is what *mechanically preserves Law 1.*)
- **Self-consistency** ⇒ no paradox-heroics; you receive the past/future like a memory you can't alter.

## 7. Open dials (decide as cycles firm up)
- **Determinism vs free will** (Law 6 / hope). Working answer: the **past is walkable-but-fixed** (sim mode); the **present is where choice lives** (real-time / your own body). Free will operates in what the channel *doesn't* say; **the only truly free act is the unrecorded one** (which is also the only un-collectable, un-predictable one → the "unwitnessed" underground).
- **Where each reveal lands:** capture/telepresence = near-future texture from **C2**; the mesh's scope = **C2**; the Processor & builders = **C3/C5**; the Gate-as-telepresence truth = a **deep** retro-fit (C3/C5). Book 1 already *shows* the mythic forms unnamed.
