# Canon Ledger — The Lighthouse

The four artifacts this branch returns to the trunk (see
[canon & continuity](../../../series-bible/canon-and-continuity.md)). Seeded from the
outline; refine against the finished draft.

## 1. Confirmed canon
_Facts this story makes objectively true for the universe._
- Interstellar navigation runs on **route-memory**: accumulated remembered crossings held
  by **beacon-stations**. Ships take **soundings** before a crossing; every passage leaves
  **residue** the beacon keeps. Losing route-memory blinds live ships (Law 1 made
  mechanical).
- A **reconstruction** assembled from residue can converse, want, and choose. Whether it
  is a *person* is not settled — but it is not nothing.
- The keeper's office transmits memory keeper-to-keeper; keepers surrender their surnames
  to the station. Some part of a keeper is the station's standing pattern.

## 2. Cultural beliefs
_Claims characters believe but that are NOT verified as true._
- That the beacons were built for ships (they may merely tolerate them).
- That a keeper's inherited knowledge is only knowledge — not self.
- That the southern relay is dead infrastructure.
- Spacers' custom: never speak the name of a lost ship inside a beacon ("the beacon
  remembers enough").

## 3. Open mysteries
_Questions deliberately left unresolved._
- How much of Edda — of any keeper — is the station's pattern.
- What the *Anthem* met, three hundred and twelve years ago.
- The single flash from the southern relay (the reader knows: Book 2's Signal; Edda never
  learns; the page never says).
- Whether the voice was Joren, the Anthem's crew, both, or neither.

## 4. Continuity hooks
_People, systems, places, symbols another story may echo._
- The **beacon network** and its **route-memory** — infrastructure any later book can
  travel by (or lose).
- The **fragment-carriage rite**: passing ships each carry a piece of a lost ship's memory
  outward — "coming home in many holds."
- The circle crossed by a single line, worn into the beacon's oldest seal.
- A fragment-ship logged bound for **Meret** (→ Book 4).
- The **Skerry**, **Edda**, **Pell**, the **Log** — available for echoes.
