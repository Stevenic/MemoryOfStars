## Premise

A beacon keeper receives a message from a ship lost centuries ago. The voice belongs to
someone the keeper knows cannot be alive. Recovering the complete transmission may require
destroying the route memories that thousands of ships depend upon.

This expands the setting beyond one world and introduces distance, navigation, isolation,
and the residue left by travelers.
