# The Last Witness — Synopsis

**Spoiler-complete.** Keep in sync with the [series bible](../../../series-bible/) and the
[canon ledger](canon-ledger.md).

A salvage expedition reaches a system no route-memory anywhere remembers — space itself
was made to forget it. Odri, forty-four, salvage-archaeologist, is of the Meret diaspora:
she grew up in the silence after the Mercy, among people who chose not to carry. On a
moonlet too smooth to be natural, behind a threshold worn from the inside, the crew find
chambers in identical rows holding more than a billion niches — and their counts of the
rows disagree. Then the singing starts: each of them hears it as a song from their own
childhood. For Odri it is shaped like the lullaby Meret deleted.

The intelligence that speaks calls itself by its function: it witnesses. It has held the
final memories of a vanished species — the humans come to call them **the Choir** — for a
million years, alone. It makes an offer priced like a trap: everything the Choir knew, in
exchange for carriage — living humans taking the stored minds into living memory. The
crew splits. Then the investigation turns the book over: the Witness is dying. Whole
strata of the Choir are already dark. The offer is not a bargain; it is triage. The
monster is a hospice, and the deadline is real.

The Choir's final consensus, played once, asked not for resurrection but remembrance —
carriage is custody, and whether carried memory is the person is argued by the crew and
ruled on by no one. Odri, whose people set their burden down, is the one who takes this
one up: she accepts the Choir's own last witness, the one who stayed to close the doors,
and receives a million-year sunset over a sea no human ever saw. The cost is permanent.
She will dream in two species now.

They leave the Witness dimming by its own choice, its duty passing. Odri — the last
witness now of the Choir, and at last of Meret's silence — records her own testimony for
the first time. At the threshold, worn into stone older than the Choir themselves, is a
circle crossed by a single line. No one comments. Homeward, with other ships already
turning toward the re-remembered roads, she teaches young Dov the words for what they
are, and the cycle ends on the farewell said to the stars: *"Come home carrying more
people than yourself."*
