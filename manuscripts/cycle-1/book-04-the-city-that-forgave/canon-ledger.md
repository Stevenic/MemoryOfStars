# Canon Ledger — The City That Forgave

The four artifacts this branch returns to the trunk (see
[canon & continuity](../../../series-bible/canon-and-continuity.md)). Seeded from the
outline; refine against the finished draft.

## 1. Confirmed canon
_Facts this story makes objectively true for the universe._
- Inherited **experiential memory** can be institutionalized at civic scale: the **Bearer**
  lines of Meret relive ancestors' preserved experience through the **Wells**.
- Deliberate, consensual **deletion of inherited memory from a whole people** is possible
  and survivable — **the Mercy**. The universe now contains a city that forgave by
  forgetting. Preservation is not always virtuous.
- The Mercy's own law: one sealed copy is kept, unread, by a custodian — who undergoes
  the Forgetting like everyone else. **The custodian keeps the door, not the memory**;
  the office is faith in one's own sealed past self.
- The two Wells of Meret share a single root: one instrument, two mouths.

## 2. Cultural beliefs
_Claims characters believe but that are NOT verified as true._
- Each bank believes its record of the Bridge Night is the true one (both are "true";
  neither is complete).
- That the Wells were dug by the city's founders.
- That the Mercy removed the grief (it removed the object of grief; Ch 8 shows mourning
  without a name).
- That the sealed copy will never be needed.

## 3. Open mysteries
_Questions deliberately left unresolved._
- What actually happened at the pivot instant of the Bridge Night — never adjudicated;
  both records true, irreconcilable.
- The **unheld figure**: someone helping people off the bridge whom neither bank's line
  claims and neither record can hold (a series motif; rhymes with Sael, never identified).
- Who built the Wells, and what else their shared root touches.
- What it does to a person to keep the sealed copy (Juna's silence is the last line's
  weight, not an answer).

## 4. Continuity hooks
_People, systems, places, symbols another story may echo._
- **Meret**, the river **Sil**, Northbank/Southbank, the rebuilt bridge.
- **The Mercy** as precedent — any later book can cite "Meret's choice."
- The **Meret diaspora**, carrying the silence outward (→ Book 5's POV).
- The circle crossed by a single line: the Wells' copings, the casket's seal.
- A Bearer family keeps an *Anthem* fragment as an heirloom (← Book 3, one line).
- The custodial rule, quotable: *"We keep it so no one must."*
- **Garrel's petition**, refused — the precedent that the sealed room stays sealed even
  for the one who was right.
- The **river lullaby** of Meret — sung whole before the Mercy; a verse fewer after
  (→ Book 5).
