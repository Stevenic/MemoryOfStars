## Premise

Two populations have preserved perfect experiential records of a centuries-old atrocity.
Each generation relives the suffering of its ancestors, making reconciliation impossible.
A reformer proposes deleting portions of both cultures' inherited memory.

This prevents the series from assuming that preservation is always virtuous. Memory can
provide identity, but it can also perpetuate hatred.
