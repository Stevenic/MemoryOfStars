# The First Pilgrim — Outline

**Memory of Stars — Novella #1** · **Length:** 28,550 words (actual draft) · **Structure:**
Prologue + 3 acts × 3 chapters + Epilogue — the founding shape. (Book 1 established it;
later books choose their own grids — the length is the law, not the grid.)

> **Status: published draft (show-don't-name revision, 2026).** This outline is a **record
> of the book as written**, held to the same template as the cycle's plans so the five
> books can be steered as one set. Changing anything here means re-drafting published
> prose — propose it, don't assume it.

- **Facet:** memory as **inheritance**.
- **Central question:** *What must we inherit before we can call ourselves adults?*
- **The book's job:** found the universe — one town, one rite, one mountain — and teach
  the reader how this series works: warmth before wonder, wonder before spectacle,
  mystery that respects them. Everything later cycles enlarge is planted here only as
  ritual and myth (the Gate, the Pilgrimage, the erasure spoken of in lowercase).
- **POV:** Lyra — seventeen, of Orin, chosen for the Pilgrimage.
- **The spine in one line:** a girl climbs a mountain to receive her town's memories and
  comes home carrying more people than herself — having briefly been Amarin, and having
  met the woman no record can hold.

## The threads
- **New truth:** the Pilgrimage is real — memory can be preserved, inherited, and briefly
  *walked into*; the Gate accepts and returns. (Rendered entirely as rite, never as tech.)
- **The mystery kept:** what the Gate is, who Sael was in full, why the erasure exists —
  all held open. The plaque answers the only question the book actually asked: whether
  one name could be kept.
- **The cost:** Elias's reunion has a price, and the memories Lyra carries home are
  already becoming dreams. Nothing is kept whole; keeping at all is the victory.
- **The promise outward:** the epilogue's calling tower and the child under another sky —
  a deliberately unpaid, series-scale promise. *(⚑ D3: Cycle 2 reserves the slot; no
  Cycle 1 book touches it.)*

## Characters in play
- **Lyra** — the chosen Pilgrim; the one who keeps asking the questions the town has
  taught itself to stop asking.
- **Tomas** — her friend, who knows everything about the rite secondhand; the ordinary
  life the extraordinary one is measured against.
- **Mara** — the kitchen the stakes are for: bread, thyme, the two bowls (the cycle's
  domestic anchor, named after the fact — never engineered).
- **Elias** — the old man who climbs every year to ask the same question; his failing
  memory is the book's first wrongness and his reunion its first price.
- **Sael** — beyond the Gate: the woman no record can hold, who divided her own
  connections so the erasure's work would stay incomplete.

## The Writer's Test (passed in draft)
- [x] New truth — the Pilgrimage works; memory can be inherited and walked into. *(Acts II–III.)*
- [x] A mystery left — the Gate's nature, Sael's fullness, the erasure, the calling tower. *(Ch 8, Epilogue.)*
- [x] A character permanently changed — Lyra: she carries more people than herself, and the carrying is already becoming dreams. *(Ch 9.)*
- [x] Civilization changed — Orin hangs a plaque; a name is publicly kept for the first time. *(Ch 9.)*
- [x] Larger universe — another sky, a tower that calls, a star she has never seen. *(Epilogue.)*

## Laws & mystery discipline
- **Law 6:** Lyra is chosen by the town's rota, not by destiny; anyone's year could have
  been this year. The extraordinary happens *to* an ordinary person who answers.
- **Law 7:** the wonder is the Gate and what lies beyond it — approached through a week of
  bread and festivals so it lands as awe, not spectacle.
- **Intentionally undefined, preserved:** the erasure appears only in lowercase, as folk
  dread; the Gate's builders, Sael's full nature, and the other sky are never explained.
  This is the only book whose public text may name "the erasure" at all — every private
  layer stays in `planning/`.

## Continuity
- **Timeline:** **CR 0** — Lyra's Pilgrimage *defines* the series' dating frame
  (`series-bible/timeline.md`).
- **Motifs planted (verbatim in later books):** the farewell — *"Come home carrying more
  people than yourself"* — and its two-finger gesture; the red bird and its rules; the
  SAEL plaque; Seren, who never sleeps and has been "several different men" (the
  continuity witness, unexplained until late cycles); Mara's kitchen.
- **Weight of names (doctrine, retro-fitted):** Sael's name is written in exactly one
  place; Orin's *world* is never named — a naming debt deliberately unpaid.
- **Hooks outward:** the calling tower and the child (⚑ D3 → Cycle 2); Master Rian and
  the minor cast (Cycle 2's vanishing-name candidates); the Ash family name → Book 2.
