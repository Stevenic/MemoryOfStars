## Structural summary

### Prologue — The Mountain Remembers
Elias makes his annual climb to the Gate and asks the question that has haunted him for
decades. The Archive offers no answers—only a promise to return in seven days. As he
leaves, he realizes he can no longer remember whether he climbed the mountain alone.

### Act I — The Last Ordinary Week (10.5k)
Readers fall in love with Orin before anything extraordinary happens. We meet Lyra, Tomas,
Mara, and Elias, experience the traditions surrounding the Pilgrimage, and begin to notice
the first subtle cracks in memory. The act ends with the Festival of Last Things and the
approaching departure.

### Act II — The Pilgrimage (11k)
The mystery deepens as Elias's memories begin to fail in impossible ways, Lyra discovers
inconsistencies within preserved memories, and the Pilgrims begin their ascent. The
mountain gradually shifts from a physical place into something stranger, culminating in
the approach to the Gate.

### Act III — The Gate (8.5k)
The mountain tests each Pilgrim through memory itself. Lyra encounters the impossible, the
Gate opens, and she awakens in another life as Amarin beneath an alien sky. The novella
closes with the first hint that Lyra and Amarin now share a single consciousness, while
the Empty Chair reveals that someone has been forgotten by history itself.

### Epilogue — Another Sky
A brief coda from Amarin's perspective establishes the mystery that will drive the next
novella. The reader understands that the Pilgrimage is not a rite of passage—it is the
beginning of a much larger story spanning worlds, memories, and forgotten lives.
