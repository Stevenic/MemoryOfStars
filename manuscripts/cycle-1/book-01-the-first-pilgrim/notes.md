## Cast
- **Lyra** — POV; the pilgrim. Awakens as Amarin.
- **Elias** — elder; makes the annual climb; his memory fails in impossible ways.
- **Tomas** — "Tomas Knows Everything" (ch. 2).
- **Mara** — of Orin.
- **Amarin** — the life Lyra awakens into beneath an alien sky; shares one consciousness with Lyra.

## Places & systems
- **Orin** — the town; the community whose rite is the Pilgrimage.
- **The Mountain** — begins physical, becomes something stranger.
- **The Gate / the Archive** — the ancient intelligence; lets pilgrims experience selected memories of the dead; insists a certain memory does not exist.

## Threads to track
- **The Empty Chair** — someone forgotten by history itself.
- Elias's decades-old question to the Archive.
- The alien sky / Amarin — the hook into Novella #2.
