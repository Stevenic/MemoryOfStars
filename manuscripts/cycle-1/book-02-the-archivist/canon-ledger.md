# Canon Ledger — The Archivist

The four artifacts this branch returns to the trunk (see
[canon & continuity](../../../series-bible/canon-and-continuity.md)). Seeded from the
outline and the drafted Act I; refine against the finished draft.

## 1. Confirmed canon
_Facts this story makes objectively true for the universe._
- Plurality is **engineered infrastructure**, not folk wisdom: the Hall of Names and the
  Resonance Lens are two nodes of one designed network. (Who designed it: not answered.)
- The **Resonance Lens** shows *relationships*, not truth; forcing a single authoritative
  record collapses plural memories — and collapse **erases**. The perfect record is the
  most destructive instrument the series has yet shown.
- The **Ash line** carried: Kieran's father was one of Sael's carriers. The record cannot
  hold Sael because she *gave herself away* — division was her act, not her fate.
- The network **answers**: a distant system responded to the Hall–Lens resonance; the
  northern beacon flashed once. The infrastructure is not inert.
- ***The Histories*** exists: Valen's practice of history is plural now; the Lens is shut
  down by choice.

## 2. Cultural beliefs
_Claims characters believe but that are NOT verified as true._
- The Society's creed: that one true, complete record is history's highest good (the
  book's engine, and its warning).
- That the Lens was "found, not made" — believed in Valen; its provenance never settled.
- That contradiction in testimony means someone is lying.

## 3. Open mysteries
_Questions deliberately left unresolved._
- Who built the network — and how many nodes it has. *(Intentionally undefined.)*
- What Sael divided herself *into*, and why the network needed her to.
- How many other carriers, in how many ages — the question closing the case opened;
  *The Histories*' first standing entry.
- What answered — and what the beacon's single flash will wake. *(→ Book 3, decades on.)*

## 4. Continuity hooks
_People, systems, places, symbols another story may echo._
- **The Histories** — the plural practice; a scholar-order sensibility later cycles can
  inherit. *(→ Cycle 3's Listeners.)*
- The **northern beacon's flash**, crawling the relays. *(→ Book 3's prologue — not the
  same night; the same wave.)*
- "The woman the record cannot hold" — Sael as a case file any archive may cite.
- The **red bird** as the great experiment; carriers in other ages implied.
- **Kieran Ash**, **Renn**, **Neris**, **Nio**, **Valen**, the **Society** — available
  for echoes, not sequels.
