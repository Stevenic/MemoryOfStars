# The Whole Toll

*(working title, author's — companion volume)*

- **Type:** companion volume — six short stories, one per cycle; the **front door** to
  the legendarium (six stories → thirty novellas → successor works).
- **Status:** Outlined ([outline.md](outline.md)).
- **Length:** ~30,400 words total (~5,000 per story + a thin Archive frame) — one
  novella's length that buys the whole universe. Each story is one sitting.
- **Frame:** presented in-universe as an Archive artifact — a record compiled after the
  fact by something that has already counted the whole toll. The compiler is never
  identified. The **Toll** = the count of years since the First Bell *and* the price;
  the title means both.
- **Spoiler posture:** **FULL-ARC** by default (story 6 includes the resolution, felt
  from outside, unexplained). **THRESHOLD** variant documented as a switch on story 6
  alone — final movement, one headnote, one afterword; nothing else changes.
- **Reading order:** designed to be read first; equally valid after or between the
  thirty; within the volume, in order — the escalation is the plot.

## The four design laws (settled)

1. **Each story is new** — never a compression or retelling of its cycle's five books.
2. **Each story carries exactly three things:** its cycle's argument, its cycle's era
   and scale, and exactly one beat of the six-beat escalation. No fourth job.
3. **Uncentered cast** — every POV is someone the novellas do not centre; a later
   reader finds nothing pre-empted, only things that already mean something.
4. **Knowing the ending is not explaining the mystery** — effects and consequences
   only; nothing on the intentionally-undefined list is answered; the keeper's keeper
   is that phrase alone, frame-only.
