# Manuscripts

The 30 novellas of **Memory of Stars**, organized as **6 cycles of 5**. Each book lives in
its own folder with `manuscript.md`, `outline.md`, `synopsis.md`, `canon-ledger.md`, and
`notes.md`.

- **New book:** copy [`_template-book/`](_template-book/) into the right cycle.
- **Titles:** folders become `book-NN-title-slug` once titled; numbering is global (01–30).
- **Continuity:** every synopsis and canon ledger must agree with the [series bible](../series-bible/).
- **Backlog of premises:** [`story-seeds.md`](story-seeds.md).
