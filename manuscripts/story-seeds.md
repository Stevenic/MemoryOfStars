# Story Seeds

A working backlog of novella premises. Each explores a **facet of memory** (the universe's
organizing force — see [foundation.md](../series-bible/foundation.md#design-decision--memory-as-force-not-plot-device)),
never simply "memory loss" repeated. Every accepted book must clear
[The Writer's Test](../series-bible/seven-laws.md#the-writers-test).

> **Cycle 1 is defined** — its five books ([*Fragments of the First Memory*](cycle-1/))
> already claim the facets *inheritance, autonomy, identity, burden, survival*. The seeds
> below are candidates for **Cycles 2–6**.

## Starting seeds (from the foundation)

| Seed | Facet of memory | Assigned to |
|------|-----------------|-------------|
| A civilization that remembers **too much** and cannot forgive. | The weight of perfect memory | — |
| An AI burdened by millions of years of accumulated experience. | Memory as burden across deep time | — |
| A colony that intentionally erases its history every generation to prevent tyranny. | Forgetting as deliberate policy | — |
| A starship whose crew inherits the memories of previous captains. | Inherited / transmitted memory | — |
| A religion built around preserving oral histories after digital archives failed. | Memory as faith and stewardship | — |
| An explorer uncovering evidence humanity already had — and forgot — a golden age. | Rediscovered / lost history | — |
