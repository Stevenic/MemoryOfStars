## Premise

Carath is young, proud, and loud — promoted to hub by the treaty's re-routed relays, one
famous name everywhere. Then the counts disagree, a stone goes blank, and a woman loses
her street's name out of her own mouth mid-sentence. The first witnessed event of its
kind; the guild's responders arrive with a protocol assembled from the patient mail; and
beneath the Hall of Rolls, something older than the city wakes up and fights.
