# Canon Ledger — Where the Names Go

The four artifacts this branch returns to the trunk (see
[canon & continuity](../../../series-bible/canon-and-continuity.md)). Seeded from the
outline; refine against the finished draft.

## 1. Confirmed canon
_Facts this story makes objectively true for the universe._
- **The erasure is active in the present, and was witnessed** — real time, on a
  thriving world. (The folk word, lowercase, is the only name it gets; its nature is
  not explained. It takes *handles before things*: signs, stones, records, then
  mouths.)
- **Something fights it.** Carath's oldest substructure woke, stalled the taking for
  one district and one hour at visible cost, and guttered. A defense exists; it is
  old; it spends itself.
- **Plural custody works** — demonstrated at street scale: what was singly held went;
  what was plurally held survived, every instance.
- **The letters from tomorrow were sent by watchers within the network's own
  architecture** — they can post ahead of need; they cannot steer; they sign nothing.
  Who and what they are: unknown.
- The **plural-name custom** exists: Carath's survivors take multiple names, on
  purpose, and the practice is spreading.

## 2. Cultural beliefs
_Claims characters believe but that are NOT verified as true._
- "Weather with a preference" — the responders' working description of the event.
- That the loud, singular, and complete are what it comes for (Book 8's pattern,
  believed by everyone who lived the night; still unproven as *rule*).
- That the letters saved the city (the crowd's courage did; the letters taught it).

## 3. Open mysteries
_Questions deliberately left unresolved._
- What the erasure *is*. Untouched.
- What woke beneath the Hall of Rolls, why now, and what its hour cost it.
- Who the watchers are. *(→ Cycle 3's question.)*
- Where the names go — the title's question, pointedly never answered.

## 4. Continuity hooks
_People, systems, places, symbols, or sayings another story may echo._
- **The Night of Strangers** and the plural-name custom — folk armor any later world
  can inherit.
- The responders' **rite-book**, now proven; the guild's protocol will spread.
- The final letter: a three-centuries-dark relay that *posts*, holding a taken name
  whole on the far side. *(← Book 1's silver tower; the child under another sky
  remains unspent.)*
- The scholar-order that interviews old minds, holding the Carath report. *(→ Cycle 3.)*
- **Ivet** (keeper of the new register), **Noor**, **Marek**, the child — available
  for echoes.
