# Canon Ledger — The Cartographer of Silence

The four artifacts this branch returns to the trunk (see
[canon & continuity](../../../series-bible/canon-and-continuity.md)). Seeded from the
outline; refine against the finished draft.

## 1. Confirmed canon
_Facts this story makes objectively true for the universe._
- The mesh has **people** in it: beacons hold the great routes; the **Wayfinders'
  Guild's route-holders** carry the small ones in living memory — licensed, tested,
  historically one holder to a road. Reach is existence: a world out of the almanac
  functionally isn't.
- **Plural holding works and is now precedent**: Farrow has four holders of record —
  ratified by the guild that calls it dilution.
- Farrow's silence was **two-sided**: the galaxy was forgetting the way in, and Farrow
  was forgetting the way out. What cut the route is never established.
- The **patient mail** exists: letters, held by the guild's dead-letter office, whose
  send-stamps haven't happened yet. Mundane, real, unexplained.

## 2. Cultural beliefs
_Claims characters believe but that are NOT verified as true._
- The guild's doctrine that a failed route means a failing holder.
- That *dilution* (plural holding) degrades a route's worth.
- That the patient mail is a filing error nobody wants to own.

## 3. Open mysteries
_Questions deliberately left unresolved._
- What took the missing mile — and why the frontier's thin threads first.
- Who sends the patient mail. *(Raised here; the cycle answers what it can in Book 10.)*
- How many silences Veya's map will hold by the time someone believes it.

## 4. Continuity hooks
_People, systems, places, symbols, or sayings another story may echo._
- The **Wayfinders' Guild** — chapter-houses, almanac, licenses, dead-letter office
  (the cycle's connective institution).
- The **almanac count** (*11,406 worlds answering*) — a number later books can watch.
- The **map of silences** — growing, deniable, filed with the patient mail (→ Book 10).
- **Farrow's four holders** — the plural-custody precedent, citable.
- A colony's route-holding contract the guild refused over its forget-clause (→ Book 7).
- **Veya**, **Osten**, **Bri**, **Cobb**, **Darro**, **Etta** — available for echoes.
