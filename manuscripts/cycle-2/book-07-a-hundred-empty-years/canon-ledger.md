# Canon Ledger — A Hundred Empty Years

The four artifacts this branch returns to the trunk (see
[canon & continuity](../../../series-bible/canon-and-continuity.md)). Seeded from the
outline; refine against the finished draft.

## 1. Confirmed canon
_Facts this story makes objectively true for the universe._
- Deliberate civilizational forgetting exists as **sustainable practice**, not crisis:
  Sowen's centennial Emptying — calendared, survivable, culture-shaping (no surnames,
  no statues, law without precedent).
- The universe now holds **both kinds of forgetting**: Meret's irreversible (the Mercy)
  and Sowen's reversible (export, not destruction). Sowen was founded ~CR 200 by the
  Mercy's emigrant children under one law: *never again unrecoverable.*
- The **Keeper's hygiene**: whoever holds the address emigrates with it — Keepers leave;
  that is why there is always a young one.
- One decade of Sowen's past is **gone for real** — burned at the first Hundredth;
  nothing on any shelf anywhere holds it.

## 2. Cultural beliefs
_Claims characters believe but that are NOT verified as true._
- "We forget to prevent tyranny" — true, and the *second* reason.
- That the Emptying destroys (it exports; after Tilde's reform, the town knows).
- That an unrecorded century was an empty one (the title's irony; the living years were
  full — only the record is empty).

## 3. Open mysteries
_Questions deliberately left unresolved._
- What the burned crate held. No one will ever know; that is the point.
- The envelope stamped with the next Hundredth's date — the patient mail again,
  unexplained here.
- Whether honesty holds for a second century — whether a practice can survive its own
  truth twice.

## 4. Continuity hooks
_People, systems, places, symbols, or sayings another story may echo._
- **Sowen** and **the Emptying** — the reversible-forgetting precedent, citable beside
  "Meret's choice."
- The **buyer of last resort** — the Librarian, named as an address only (→ Book 8).
- **Joss** and the consignment: ten crates, one urn, one passenger (→ Book 8's opening).
- The founders' law, quotable: *"Never again unrecoverable."*
- **Tilde** — a Keeper abroad with a head full of a century (available for echoes).
- Backward: the Mercy and its diaspora (← Book 4); the guild's refused forget-clause
  contract (← Book 6, one line).
