## Premise

The powers fight by moving beacons: drag a chain and a system falls out of hearing —
annexation by silence, all of it legal because the provenance registry is gone. When the
next move will make the Selt unhearable, a crew of exactly the wrong people commits the
war's only generous crime: an illegal act of *placement*. The wit runs high; the bill
arrives in the fourth movement, and it is paid in full.
