# Canon Ledger — The Severed Reach

The four artifacts this branch returns to the trunk (see
[canon & continuity](../../../series-bible/canon-and-continuity.md)). Seeded from the
outline; refine against the finished draft.

## 1. Confirmed canon
_Facts this story makes objectively true for the universe._
- **Reach is territory.** Beacons can be moved; treaty range is capped (lit hops are
  zero-sum); powers annex by silence. Cutting a people's last transmitter subtracts
  them from the conversation without a shot.
- With the provenance registry gone (← Book 8), **no beacon placement is provable** —
  the war's legal engine.
- **Placement-as-right** enters treaty language: the Selt's illegal hop, legalized by
  surrendered range — a precedent any silenced people can now cite.
- The keepers' **residue-craft** (recovering a dead route) is real, works, and is now
  written into the Skerry order's liturgy.
- A beacon's old **station-voice** can wake and keep its own log.

## 2. Cultural beliefs
_Claims characters believe but that are NOT verified as true._
- Each power's certainty that the other moved first.
- That the war is ordinary politics (the reader, holding Books 6–8, may suspect the
  powers fear silence for reasons none of them could name).
- The guild's public position that it was never involved.

## 3. Open mysteries
_Questions deliberately left unresolved._
- Who moved first — and why silence, of all weapons, is the one everyone reaches for.
- What the station-voice's log now holds, and who else it has ever logged as crew.

## 4. Continuity hooks
_People, systems, places, symbols, or sayings another story may echo._
- **The Selt**, audible again — their own name plural and untranslated, kept off the
  page (weight of names).
- **Carath's promotion** in the treaty annex (→ Book 10's premise).
- The **crown route** under plural holders — Book 6's heresy, now the reach's richest
  road's ordinary practice.
- **Lio** (delisted, honest), **Onna's order**, **Madge**, **Quill**, **Ru** —
  available for echoes.
- Backward: the released registry (← Book 8); the keepers' craft (← Book 3).
