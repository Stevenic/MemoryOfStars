# Canon Ledger — The Last Librarian

The four artifacts this branch returns to the trunk (see
[canon & continuity](../../../series-bible/canon-and-continuity.md)). Seeded from the
outline; refine against the finished draft.

## 1. Confirmed canon
_Facts this story makes objectively true for the universe._
- The galaxy has an **attic**: the Librarian — an archive-mind older than nations
  (human-built, settled-ages old), buyer of last resort for what civilizations can
  neither hold nor destroy. Its own name was the first thing it deleted.
- A mind can practice **triage on itself**: deletion and dispersal as deliberate,
  reasoned acts of care — at the price of parts of its self.
- **The dispersal happened**: the irreplaceables of the reach now sit on the **long
  shelf** — hundreds of minor keepers — and *no complete catalog of where anything went
  exists, by design*. Some things are safe now precisely because they can no longer be
  found.
- The old **beacon-registry** — provenance of half the settled reach's placements — was
  released into obscurity in the triage. *(→ Book 9: nothing is provable now.)*

## 2. Cultural beliefs
_Claims characters believe but that are NOT verified as true._
- **The Librarian's pattern** — that its most complete, most singular collections are
  the ones that later cannot be found; that what is whole in one place gets *taken*.
  Centuries of correlation, no proof, dismissed as senescence. *(The book argues it;
  Book 10 witnesses it. Until then it is a claim.)*
- The trustees' diagnosis: that the mind is failing rather than choosing.
- That a great hall is the safest place a treasure can live.

## 3. Open mysteries
_Questions deliberately left unresolved._
- What takes the complete things — never shown, never named here.
- The three letters "not yet due" — kept, undelivered, unexplained.
- What the traveler was looking for on the long shelf — held once, findable never.

## 4. Continuity hooks
_People, systems, places, symbols, or sayings another story may echo._
- **The long shelf** — the reach's memory as ten thousand parishes; any later book can
  visit one shelf of it (→ Book 10's plural-copied rolls ride it).
- The released **beacon-registry** (→ Book 9's war).
- The Librarian's testimony, quotable: *a trust that outlives its beneficiaries is a
  mausoleum.*
- **Joss** — the courier of the dispersal; **Hale** and **Wynn's bequest**; **the
  Librarian**, smaller and alive — available for echoes.
- Backward: Sowen's crates and Tilde (← Book 7); dispersal-as-survival (← Book 3's
  *Anthem*, the same shape at archive scale, never cited).
