## Premise

The galaxy has an attic: a buyer of last resort where civilizations send what they can
neither hold nor destroy. Its keeper — an archive-mind older than nations, whose own
name was the first thing it deleted — has noticed something about its most complete
collections, and begun making itself plural, scattered, and boring before something
finishes the job. Stewardship, rendered exactly: what the keeper owes the kept, paid
out of what the keeper is.
