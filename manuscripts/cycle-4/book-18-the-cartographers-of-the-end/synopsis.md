# The Cartographers of the End — Synopsis

**Spoiler-complete.** Keep in sync with the [series bible](../../../series-bible/) and the
[canon ledger](canon-ledger.md).

The prologue teaches the institute's **ledger** in one page — its three states:
**answering** (a world in the mesh's conversation), **relay-held** (answering by plural
bookkeeping alone), **attested** (existing only in surviving reference, where Breda's
three entries sit in a careful hand). The count has never gone down. Tonight's agenda
item three.

Tess, a senior mapper who surveys forward only — never a re-verification, never old
ground — draws the assignment nobody wants: re-verify Penna, the green blank, because her
papers say *born: Penna* — the one qualification the blank has already taken from her.
In the committee room the vote passes the first decrement in the count's history — one
world struck from answering to attested while the room holds its breath. Tess accepts the
commission the same hour.

Penna exists — orchards gone feral, roads good, roofs sound, sunlight on a world no
memory will hold, evacuated a generation ago as the records thinned. A local guide meets
the survey at the bend as if expected. By nightfall Marn, her partner, must re-teach
himself the day's place-names from his notes; Tess's notes are already wrong. The region
will not stay written — entries unwrite, photographs mis-file, the theodolite drops fixes
— so Tess builds the workaround that breaks her rule: the **relay survey** — many mappers
in short shifts, plural notes reconciled daily, no single record asked to hold anything
alone. It holds.

Her papers lead to a house: fieldwork on her own childhood, pencil-marks of a growing
child on a doorframe at exactly her heights — love without memory's help. The guide walks
them to everything the survey would miss, and Marn's control-notes surface the impossible
detail: the rolls hold no one of his description, and he has been "here before" every
survey for forty years. Nobody says anything. (It is Seren; the page never says so.)

Back at the institute, Tess plots the whole cycle's data on the overlay wall, and the
selection rule resolves out of the noise: *the obscure vanish; the referenced persist;
the coherent collect cleanest.* Grier names it for the ledger's preface — and names it
wrong: an appetite, *the hunger at the end of maps*. Tess dissents in method: the relay
survey is adopted institute-wide, Penna becomes the first relay-held entry — and the
method's own law charges its inventor: sole custody forbidden, she surrenders her private
notes of the house into the relay, to be held by strangers in shifts.

The almanac's new edition: the count down, the preface wrong, the relay-held column small
and growing — and one line Tess added to the institute's oath: *we go back over old
ground.* She signs the Penna re-verification for next year. Forward-only no more.
