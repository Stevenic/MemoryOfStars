# The Falling Node — Outline

**Memory of Stars — Novella #20** · **Target length:** ~31,400 words · **Structure:**
Prologue + 3 acts × 3 chapters + Epilogue — the finale echoes the founding shape, and
this story has three true movements: the cooling, the defense, the fall.

- **Facet:** memory as **survival**.
- **Central question:** *What survives when the keeping-places fall?*
- **The book's job:** the dark cycle's capstone. A central node of the network falls
  to the Erasure — undeniable, present-tense, on ground the reader has loved since
  Book 4: **Meret**, city of the Wells, the Mercy, and the unopened room. The
  Custodian is **seen at work** at last; the cycle's budgeted answer is confirmed and
  logged; every faction's failed answer and every practiced armor meets the real
  thing at scale — and the cycle ends with the threat undeniable and C5's mandate
  set: *understand it, since fighting it has failed.*
- **POV:** Yune — fifty, **Warden of the Wells**: the office Juna's line grew into —
  keeper of the unopened room, warden of the node, the person who holds the door and
  the ledger both. Her tic: she speaks to the room she has never opened, nightly,
  through the door — three centuries of wardens have; it is the office's one
  unwritten rite.
- **The spine in one line:** when the Wells of Meret begin to cool, their warden must
  run the reach's first full defense of a falling node — every armor the series has
  built, deployed at civic scale — and, when the collector comes anyway, decide what
  a keeper of sealed things owes a failing keeper of everything.

## The threads
- **The fall:** nodes can fall. The Wells — one instrument, two mouths, older than
  the city — cool over weeks: route-soundings thin, the rolls blank faster than the
  relay teams re-copy, the almanac decrements *by districts*. The atlas shrinks on
  the page, and this time the page is home.
- **The Custodian, seen (the spend confirmed and logged):** at the node's heart,
  through the Well-mouths, the collector is finally *witnessed at work* — and the
  cycle's answer holds: **maintenance**. Not appetite; triage — dutiful, patient,
  *failing* — a caretaker culling a flooding archive with hands that shake. (The
  Shepherd's shape, at terrible scale; senile-versus-lucid stays an open dial. Who
  it is, why storage is finite, where the collected go: untouched → C5.)
- **The defense:** everything the series built, deployed: relay-held rolls (18),
  incompleteness doctrine (19), the Night of Strangers (10), plural custody in every
  register — the first full-dress test of the practice *in the collector's
  presence.*
- **The casket:** the Mercy's sealed copy — three centuries unread, the most
  coherent singular object in the city, sitting in the node's heart like a lure.
  The office's law against the cycle's rule: *the coherent collect cleanest.* Yune's
  whole book is that sentence.
- **The hoard arrives:** Breda — old now, the institute's strangest archivist —
  comes with the relief columns, carrying attested-sole-source proof of three
  collected regions… and one page she palmed thirty years ago in another life: the
  founding deed of Meret's bridge (← Book 16, Ch 2 — a long-shelf stray the diaspora
  carried out; survived by being scattered, obscure, and finally stolen).
- **Chapter-Two Rule:** chapter 2 is the cooling made undeniable — the southern
  Well-mouth goes cold mid-rite, and the almanac decrements by a district, on the
  page, in the warden's own hand.

## Characters in play
- **Yune** — the Warden. Juna's office, grown institutional; talks to the sealed
  room nightly through the door. The book asks her to end a three-century office
  correctly, and she does — twice.
- **Breda** — (← Book 16) the keeper who keeps, vindicated at scale: her sack is
  the pattern's control data and the city's strange comfort. The cycle's
  through-thread pays off in her presence.
- **Commander Neva-of-the-relays** — the responders' descendant service, running
  the civic defense; the rite-book's fourth edition in her coat.
- **Doss** — Yune's deputy, young, roll-keeper of Southbank; the book's unguarded
  heart, and the voice of the question everyone thinks: *open the room; read it
  before it's taken.*
- **The Custodian** — seen at work, named by no one on the page (the institute's
  "hunger" frame dies here; the city's word for it, coined in the fall, is **the
  keeper's keeper** — folk-naming doing what doctrine couldn't). Never spoken to;
  never speaking. Witnessed.

## The Writer's Test (must pass)
- [ ] New truth — nodes can fall and cities can survive them; the Custodian
      witnessed: **maintenance, failing** *(the cycle's budgeted answer, confirmed
      and logged here)*; the plural armor proven in the collector's presence.
      *(Ch 6–8.)*
- [ ] A mystery left — who/what the keeper's keeper is; why it fails; where the
      casket and the collected go *(untouched → C5)*; what the room would have
      said. *(Ch 6, 8.)*
- [ ] A character permanently changed — Yune: the office ended correctly; the
      warden of a door becomes the teller of the news. *(Ch 8, Epilogue.)*
- [ ] Civilization changed — the first defended node; survival mechanics proven
      and exported; the wardens' rite gone plural; the institute's frame
      corrected. *(Ch 7, 9, Epilogue.)*
- [ ] Larger universe — the defense is failing *at scale*; the collector is a
      failing keeper, which is worse than a monster; C5's mandate stands.
      *(Ch 6, Epilogue.)*

## Laws & mystery discipline
- **Canon-spend guard (the cycle's single answer, logged here):** confirmed on
  the page: the Erasure is *maintenance* — a failing caretaker's triage — and
  the Custodian is witnessed at work. **Untouched, verified:** its identity and
  origin (C5's), why storage is finite, where collected things go, the
  senile-versus-lucid dial. The city's folk-name ("the keeper's keeper") is
  belief, logged as such.
- **The Scouring guard (constitutional):** Meret falls; **Orin is not touched.**
  The protected first place stays protected until C6's scheduled beat. No draft
  may foreshadow Orin here.
- **No cheap resurrection:** the Wells stay fallen; the casket stays taken and
  stays sealed; the Mercy's deleted stays deleted. Breda's page is a *survival*,
  not a return — one page, not a past.
- **Eucatastrophe discipline:** the grace is the page (luck-shaped, lifetime-paid);
  the pass-over of the plural-held is *earned proof*, not luck, and the outline
  keeps the two distinct.
- **No chosen one:** Yune holds the office the calendar gave her; the defense is
  a city's, not a hero's; the Custodian chooses nothing — it triages.
- **Applicability guard (the series' highest-risk book for it):** the keeper's
  keeper fails in archival and mechanical terms (re-culling, stuttering triage);
  the city grieves in civic ones (a fallen well, a closed office). The two idioms
  never share a chapter's climax; the grandmother simile stays in Ch 9 and the
  Custodian's stutter in Ch 6, and no draft may move them adjacent. The failing
  is purpose-residue, not disease.
- **Draft guards:** the witnessed failure-behaviors must each admit a lucid
  reading (the re-culling can be re-culling *regenerated relay copies*) — the
  senile-vs-lucid dial stays genuinely open; inherited armors are taught one per
  scene with Doss as the cold reader's proxy (no hazing in the finale); "doing to
  a node what Verine did to the Record" must be shown in-scene, never by
  allusion; if the close strains, fold the ledger line and Grier's preface into
  Ch 9 and leave the Epilogue two beats — the mandate, and Yune telling the news
  to the city.
- **Name note (ruled deliberate):** Yune/Juna is the office-line homage — sound
  carrying where blood doesn't (the office is succession, never descent). Logged
  so no draft "fixes" it.
