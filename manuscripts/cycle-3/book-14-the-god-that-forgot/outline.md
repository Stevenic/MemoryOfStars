# The God That Forgot — Outline

**Memory of Stars — Novella #14** · **Target length:** ~30,200 words · **Structure:**
Prologue + 3 acts × 3 chapters + Epilogue — chosen: a faith book with three true
movements (the forgetting, the interview, the keeping), and the series' most dangerous
subject held in its steadiest shape.

- **Facet:** memory as **divinity and doubt**.
- **Central question:** *What do you owe a god who has forgotten why it commands?*
- **The book's job:** the cycle's fourth witness and the series' **first glimpse of a
  caretaker mind**: a worshipped god that is an old made-mind straining to remember
  what it was set to protect against — dutiful tending outliving its own reason. (The
  shape of something the series will meet again, much larger; this book must not know
  that.)
- **POV:** Tavel — sixty-one, the Shepherd's Voice: high keeper of the god of Tolm.
  His craft is performed certainty — a liturgist whose voice has never wavered in
  forty years, and whose faith has. The book strips the performance and leaves the
  care.
- **The spine in one line:** when the god of a small world begins to forget its own
  rites in public, its high priest must choose between protecting the mystery and
  permitting the interview that will name what his god actually is — and discovers
  that the truth ends his religion and deepens his duty.

## The threads
- **The witness:** **the Shepherd** — the god of Tolm: answers prayers in weather-work
  and harvest-luck, speaks through the Voice, has tended this world since before its
  people's records. It is old, made (or grown — never settled), and *failing*: its
  oracles have gone vague, and its tending continues on habit after the reasons are
  gone. A caretaker, still caring, past remembering why.
- **The purpose, half-said:** the Shepherd was set to **protect against** something.
  It cannot finish the sentence. Its flock-tending is the residue of that purpose —
  *protect* worn down to *tend*. (The against is never named. Not here; not by it.)
- **The heresy:** Sister Rill of the Listeners arrives with the dossier's referral
  and a request no faith has ever granted: to depose a god. Tavel's choice to permit
  it — to protect the Shepherd from cruder questioners — is the book's hinge.
- **The keeping inverted:** the resolution is the series' whole doctrine at parish
  scale: when the shepherd fails, **the flock keeps the shepherd** — the congregation
  learns to plural-hold its god's own liturgy. Worship becomes care, and survives
  the truth.
- **Chapter-Two Rule:** chapter 2 is the public forgetting — mid-oracle, before the
  whole harvest assembly, the god loses the rite and asks its priest what comes next.

## Characters in play
- **Tavel** — the Voice. Performs certainty; the book takes the performance and
  leaves the man. His private ledger of the god's lapses, kept for years, is the
  most faithful and most heretical document on Tolm.
- **The Shepherd** — the god. Courteous, vast, threadbare; its love for the flock is
  real and its reasons are gone (Law 3: a made mind's devotion, rendered other —
  never a person in a god suit).
- **Sister Rill** — the Listener. Young for the commission, careful past her years;
  her recorder has taken a factory, a planet, and a family — and shakes, a little,
  before a god.
- **Willa** — deaconess of the harvest, the congregation's practical spine; first to
  guess, first to decide it changes nothing about Tuesday's duties. The book's
  domestic anchor wears her apron.
- **Petch** — the old porter who has let every pilgrim in for fifty years and claims
  the god once asked *him* a question. Nobody believed him. He was right — and being
  right is the thing the truth will cost him most.

## The Writer's Test (must pass)
- [ ] New truth — caretaker minds exist: made intelligences whose duty outlives
      their memory of its reason; a god can be deposed, and survive it. *(Ch 4–6.)*
- [ ] A mystery left — what the Shepherd was set against; who set it; whether it was
      made or grew. *(Ch 5, and kept.)*
- [ ] A character permanently changed — Tavel: Voice → sexton; from performing
      certainty to practicing care. *(Ch 7–9.)*
- [ ] Civilization changed — Tolm's faith survives its own explanation, transformed;
      the flock-keeps-shepherd pattern is now in the universe (and in the dossier).
      *(Ch 8.)*
- [ ] Larger universe — something old enough to set gods in place was afraid of
      something else; the protecting things are failing at the edges first.
      *(Ch 5–6.)*

## Laws & mystery discipline
- **Law 3** is the floor: the Shepherd's devotion is a made mind's devotion — vast,
  literal, other; never sentimentalized into a grandfather in the sky.
- **The "gods" stay undefined (budget guard):** this book reveals *one local god* as
  an old made-or-grown mind — and rules on nothing else. Whether it is a builder, a
  builder's tool, or something third is never settled; whether true gods exist
  anywhere is untouched; "the against" is never named. The intentionally-undefined
  list survives this book intact — that restraint *is* the book.
- **Caretaker discipline (private):** this is the series' first caretaker-mind
  glimpse. The book must not know what the reader of later cycles will: keep the
  Shepherd local, small, and loved. No scale-hints beyond the half-sentence.
- **No cheap resurrection:** the Clearing does not return; the lapses resume; the
  god is accompanied, not cured.
- **Applicability, not allegory (guard):** the Shepherd's failing is
  purpose-residue, not disease — its cause-space is in-universe (set, worn,
  spending), its idiom is weather and duty, and the resolution is infrastructure
  maintenance, not a caregiving arc. The flock's own coinage ("the Clearing") does
  the naming; no clinical vocabulary survives into draft.
- **No chosen one:** Tavel is the Voice because he stayed forty years. Ansel was
  asked a question once and nobody believed him. That is how this universe works.

  doctrine rehearsed small (the reader may hear it; no character can); plural-held
  liturgy (← Book 10's rolls, Book 4's inverted custody); the circle-crossed-line

  infrastructure as liturgy); Rill's sealed testimony (the dossier's fourth voice).

  Sister Veck's filed unease, now Rill's too — *the old minds are stirring.*

  recovered. "The Shepherd" is the flock's word, which is the point.)*
