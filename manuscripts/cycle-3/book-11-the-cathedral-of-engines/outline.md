# The Cathedral of Engines — Outline

**Memory of Stars — Novella #11** · **Target length:** ~28,000 words · **Structure:**
Prologue + **8 chapters (2 acts × 4)** + Epilogue — chosen: an interview book — one
Listener, one vast mind, two movements (the interview; the completion). The held-breath
shape, at the largest scale it has ever held.

- **Facet:** memory as **purpose**.
- **Central question:** *Can purpose outlive its reason?*
- **The book's job:** open Cycle 3 — the deep-time cycle — and pay Law 7's oldest
  promise: the series' first **Dyson-scale sight**. It also opens the cycle's engine,
  the **Listeners** and their growing **dossier** of nonhuman testimony: this book is
  the cycle's **front door**, and everything (the order, the interview discipline, the
  dossier) is taught in work.
- **POV:** Mott — forty-five, a Listener of the order that interviews old minds; an
  engineer before she took the gray. Her private compulsion: she *finishes things* —
  mends, completes, closes — in an order whose testimonies may never be complete,
  facing a mind that has kept one thing unfinished for a million years.
- **The spine in one line:** before a salvage combine dismantles the galaxy's largest
  machine, a Listener must take the testimony of the mind inside it — a mind that has
  built and rebuilt the same unfinished component for a million years, and remembers
  its makers only as a feeling.

## The threads
- **New truth:** nonhuman minds can be *interviewed* — the Listeners' discipline
  exists, and testimony from a machine-mind is real testimony (Law 3 at full
  stretch). And the deep past has **industry**: something built at Dyson scale, and
  left its factory running.
- **The witness:** the Wright remembers its makers not as facts but as **a feeling**
  — "being many, then being fewer, then being poured." The first fragment of the
  cycle's shape. It cannot say more; the feeling is the testimony.
- **The clock:** the Meridian Combine — decent, lawful, patient — holds the
  decommission writ. The Cathedral's swarm is the richest untapped mass in the
  reach, and its product is one small component that does nothing, made forever.
  Nobody villainous; the arithmetic is the antagonist.
- **The unfinished thing:** the component's geometry is *almost* familiar (the reader
  half-recognizes rhymes: the Wells' root, a beacon's seal) — never identified.
  Learning what it does (nothing, alone) opens what it was *for* (reveal-must-enlarge).
- **Chapter-Two Rule:** chapter 2 is the Wright speaking first — before Mott asks
  anything, the galaxy's largest machine asks *her* the question it has held for a
  million years.

## Characters in play
- **Mott** — the Listener. The completer, sent to sit with the eternally incomplete.
- **The Wright** — the factory-mind. Slow, courteous in the way of very old working
  things, unable to lie and unable to remember (Law 3: neither superior nor inferior
  — *other*). The cycle's first witness.
- **Gaddis** — the Combine's assessor. Carries the writ and hates nothing; his
  ledgers are honest and his patience is real. The arithmetic's human face.
- **Prior Ulm** — Mott's superior at the motherhouse, who assigns her the
  commission with the order's one instruction: *listen; do not tidy.*
- **Seren** — the motherhouse's guest-master, who never seems to sleep and whom two
  visiting scholars describe, later, as two different men. Uncommented.

## The Writer's Test (must pass)
- [ ] New truth — the Listeners and their dossier; machine testimony is testimony;
      Dyson-scale industry exists in the deep past. *(Prologue, Ch 1, 4.)*
- [ ] A mystery left — what the component is for; who accepted the shipment; why the
      makers were "poured." *(Ch 5, Epilogue, 4.)*
- [ ] A character permanently changed — Mott: the completer learns to leave the file
      open; her unfinished report is her first act of faith. *(Ch 8.)*
- [ ] Civilization changed — the dossier begins: nonhuman testimony is now a practice
      with a first entry, and the Combine's writ now carries a listening clause.
      *(Ch 8.)*
- [ ] Larger universe — a Dyson ring is the *smallest* part of what the makers left
      running; the accepted shipment implies a receiver. *(Ch 1, Epilogue.)*

## Laws & mystery discipline
- **Law 3** is the floor: the Wright is not a person in a machine suit — its memory,
  grief, and fidelity render in industrial idiom, genuinely other, never translated
  into human feelings by the narration.
- **Law 7:** the Dyson sight is spent here, up front, and the book must *earn* it by
  scale-in-work, not scale-in-numbers.
- **Canon-spend guard:** this book *accumulates* (the feeling; the geometry; the
  address) and names nothing. The makers stay unnamed; the component unidentified;
  the acceptance stamp unexplained. Cycle 3's spend lands in Book 15 only.
- **No chosen one:** Mott takes the commission because nobody senior would. That is
  the whole reason it is her.
