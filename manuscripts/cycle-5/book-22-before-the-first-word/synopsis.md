# Before the First Word — Synopsis

**Spoiler-complete.** Keep in sync with the [series bible](../../../series-bible/) and the
[canon ledger](canon-ledger.md).

Four years into the store, beneath the golden-age horizon, the Restorers find the **deep
horizon**: strata older than any human rise. Over the Claimants' Concord's steady
petitions, the team votes to walk the oldest recoverable stratum, taken across three
sessions. Kova, the walker, carrier-trained in Odri's practice, counts the shaft's
thresholds going down: eleven. The rule that will own the midpoint is taught plainly
first: an opened stratum plays to its end whether walked or not; interrupt, and the
remainder passes unwitnessed forever.

The first deep walk is the detonation: standing inside a morning a million years deep, an
era rendered other, entire — and in it a keeper of songs counting verses the way keepers
do, finding one *gone*: not forgotten, gone, the shape of its absence still warm. The
first noticing. Above, the frame absorbs it — Amel's live transcript, Hask's
contamination tests, the ledger's first strike-through — and Kova, surfacing, counts her
thresholds and comes up one short. She says nothing.

Deeper, the noticing spreads and the era's own institutions refuse it; the keeper of
songs begins, alone and at night, to *copy* — the heresy of plural keeping, invented in
fear. Then the braid slips. A thread of the walked era fuses live into Kova's own memory:
she surfaces speaking a dead grammar, missing an hour of her childhood and holding an
hour of someone else's morning. Pim works the un-join threshold by threshold while,
below, the opened stratum burns on unwitnessed. The un-join saves her — mostly; one
walked thread stays, forever — and the vote to resume is not dutiful. It is desperate.

Re-entered mid-burn, the walk reaches the origin's heart: **the first defense** — not a
wall, not a weapon, a practice. The keeper of songs teaching the copying to others;
verses held plural; names spoken to strangers; the first deliberate scattering of what
mattered into many hands — the frightened, in the dark, inventing the doctrine out of
love. Who the first minds were stays veiled; so does what was taking the meaning.

After: the stratum spent, the transcript clean as far as testing can say, and Kova now
remembers being one of the first minds for one morning — a carrier's custody, not
contamination; twelve thresholds at every door. Advocate Yent arrives in person for the
Concord, demanding observer standing at all future walks; Hask half-agrees, which is
worse; the vote passes with conditions that will bind the next two descents. Three strata
remain below.

On the transcript's final page, the keeper of songs, teaching, says the era's word for
what the copying *is* — and Amel renders it in the only human words that fit the shape.
The page shows them. The reader knows them. Unremarked, undated, filed.
