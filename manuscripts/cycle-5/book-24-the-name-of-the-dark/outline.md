# The Name of the Dark — Outline

**Memory of Stars — Novella #24** · **Target length:** ~30,700 words · **Structure:**
Prologue + 3 acts × 3 chapters + Epilogue, re-pointed — chosen: the shape is read at
the **midpoint**; the back half is not *what is it?* but *how do we live with what it
means?* — the consequence grid, for the book where the series walks to the edge of
its own oldest silence.

- **Facet:** memory as **the unnameable**.
- **Central question:** *What do you call the thing that takes the names?*
- **The book's job:** the closest the series ever comes to naming the Erasure — and
  the discipline that makes it survivable: **the root cause is named only as
  shape.** The last dark strata hold the builders' own investigation of the dying,
  and their conclusion was never written in words, because words are collectable.
  It was *drawn*. The book ends twenty-four books of trail: **the circle crossed by
  a single line, read at last** — the oldest warning and the oldest instruction in
  one mark. And the horror underneath: the dark may be structural — even, in a
  way, a mercy.
- **POV:** Hask — fifty-three, the Restorers' skeptic: the team's tester, the one
  who has spent three books keeping beautiful contamination out of the record. His
  tic: he doubts aloud, in numbered objections; the team calls it *Hask's litany*
  and would not walk without it. The book takes the series' best doubter to the
  one finding he cannot doubt — and watches what a skeptic does with certainty.
- **The spine in one line:** walking the builders' own investigation of the dying,
  the team's skeptic follows the first minds' evidence to their unwritten
  conclusion — a shape instead of a name — and must decide what the reach is owed:
  the mark's meaning, the mercy-horror underneath it, or the doubt that keeps both
  bearable.

## The threads
- **The dark strata:** the last two pages of the store — the builders'
  investigation era: their instruments, their arguments, their mounting data on
  the dying of meaning. The series' oldest question, walked as *someone else's
  research project* — the Restorers reading the notes of researchers half a
  million years dead, on the same problem, with better data and the same dread.
- **The shape, not the name (the budget's exact line):** the builders' conclusion,
  when the team finally reaches it, is not a word. It is the mark: **the circle**
  (the whole, the coherent, the singly-kept) **crossed by a single line** (the
  cut — the cull that takes the whole; the division that saves it). One mark
  holding both readings at once — warning and instruction, wound and armor,
  drawn wherever the practice was kept because it could not be said safely.
  Twenty-four books of uncommented appearances resolve into the series' thesis,
  drawn by the first hands. *(What causes the dying: still unnamed. The mark is
  its shape, not its source. The budget holds.)*
- **The mercy-horror:** the builders' data pointed somewhere their notes approach
  and refuse: the dying may be **structural** — the condition of a finite medium
  holding infinite meaning — and the cull, gently run, may be the *mercy* form
  of a thing that would otherwise arrive as crush. The team must hold the
  possibility that the enemy of the whole series is the least bad version of
  something worse. Nobody is asked to believe it. Everybody has to carry it.
- **The vanishing name, escalated to the series' own record (device 1, instance
  #4 — a book's own account):** in Act I (Ch 3), verifying transcription drift,
  Hask reads aloud from the team's copy of the Hall's account of the First
  Pilgrimage — the reach's most-copied text — and in the quoted passage, where
  the old climber's name should stand, the line is blank. *(The reader knows the
  name. It is Elias. No character notices — none of them ever knew it.
  **Mechanics guard:** the blank matches across all copies consulted; the
  calibration read passes clean — drift is copies disagreeing, and they do not.
  Never depict the Hall or assert the original's state.)*
- **Chapter-Two Rule:** chapter 2 is the first dark walk — the builders'
  instrument hall, and the realization that reframes the cycle: *they were
  investigating too.* The makers the series has treated as answer-holders were
  themselves mid-question when they poured.

## Characters in play
- **Hask** — the skeptic. Numbered doubts, litany-dry wit, the team's spine of
  rigor; privately, the one most afraid, because doubt has always been how he
  keeps fear procedural.
- **Isla Ash** — the lead; must decide what the reach is told, and when, and how
  — a staker's instincts against a steward's duties.
- **Kova** and **Pim** — walker and joiner, the veterans now; their earned
  costs (the thread, the chord) are the team's instruments for reading the
  dark strata without breaking.
- **Amel** — the scribe; the notation problem at maximum: how do you transcribe
  a conclusion that was never worded? (Answer: you draw it. The transcript's
  last page is a mark, not a sentence.)
- **The investigators** — the builders of the research era, walked: patient,
  frightened, rigorous — the series' first scientists, recognizable across half
  a million years by the shape of their care.

## The Writer's Test (must pass)
- [ ] New truth — the builders were investigators, not answer-holders; the mark
      read at last (shape as thesis); the store spent to its end. *(Ch 2, 5, 9.)*
- [ ] A mystery left — **what causes the dying** (approached, drawn, never
      named — the budget's kept line); who cut each of the trail's marks; the
      mercy-question, held not answered. *(Ch 5–7.)*
- [ ] A character permanently changed — Hask: the doubter given certainty, who
      makes custody of it; the litany gains its unnumbered article. *(Ch 6, 8.)*
- [ ] Civilization changed — the mark's plain reading published: the practice
      has its founding text at last, and it is a drawing. *(Ch 9.)*
- [ ] Larger universe — the question is older than the answerers; the dark has
      structure; even the makers walked toward it mid-question. *(Ch 2, 5.)*

## Laws & mystery discipline
- **The budget's exact line, verified:** the root cause is named **only as
  shape**. The mark is read; the cause stays unworded on the page and in these
  files. The mercy-possibility is *held as possibility* — argued by data,
  believed by no one aloud, resolved never. If a draft names the dark, the
  draft has failed the series.
- **Symbol discipline (the trail's payoff, scoped):** this book decodes the
  mark's *meaning*; it does not identify who cut any given instance (the
  beacon seal, the Well copings, the vault threshold, the temple stone keep
  their hands unknown — some were builders, some were keepers of the practice,
  some are open forever). The trail resolves as language, not as authorship.
- **Vanishing-name discipline (instance #4 — the escalation's last listed
  step):** a book's own record — the Hall's account of Book 1, quoted with the
  blank. Uncommented, unnoticed (no living character ever knew the name), 
  reader-only, logged in the device table. The provenance-frame (device 11)
  is hereby armed and fired once; C6 inherits what it implies.
- **No prophecy:** the investigators' mark is conclusion, not foretelling; the
  team's publication is a reading, not a scripture — Isla's ruling makes the
  epistemics explicit on the page.
- **Applicability, not allegory (guard):** the mercy-horror stays at structural
  scale — a finite medium, a gentle cull — rendered in archival and cosmological
  terms only; no character maps it onto a person, and no human end-of-life
  vocabulary survives into draft. Applicable to all, reducible to none.
- **No chosen one:** Hask holds the tenth article because a doubter's custody
  is safest — an argument, not an election; he accepts under protest, which is
  the point.
