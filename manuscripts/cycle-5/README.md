# Cycle 5 — *The First Silence*

Books 21–25. The origin cycle: **the deep past, walked**. Under Cycle 4's mandate —
*understand it, since fighting it has failed* — a small recovery team finds the cold
strata: compressed memory seeded deep in the ground of things, holding humanity's
erased first rise and, beneath it, the era of the first minds. The origin is not
narrated in this cycle. It is *entered* — and every stratum is single-walk: to know
it is to spend it.

Its connective tissue breaks the series' own pattern on purpose: not an institution
but a **team** — the Restorers, five people, fallible, chartered small — and an
artifact that **empties**: the ledger of the strata is struck through book by book,
draining into the testament the finale must write.

## Central questions

1. **The Golden Age That Wasn't** — What do you owe a past nobody mourned?
2. **Before the First Word** — Who spoke first against the silence?
3. **The Makers' Grief** — Why would you become the floor instead of the fallen?
4. **The Name of the Dark** — What do you call the thing that takes the names?
5. **The Last Testament** — What do you leave when you have spent everything you found?

## Why this sequence works

The cycle descends, then bequeaths:

> **the find → the noticing → the pouring → the mark → the testament**

Each book walks one stratum deeper and spends what it walks; the store empties as
the story completes. By the end, the reader should hold the cycle's argument:

> **The past can be recovered exactly once — so what you make of it is what
> everyone after you will have.**

And the last page hands the final cycle its charge: *we spent the past; what we owe
now is the ending.*

## Shared-canon discipline

As in Cycles 1–4: each book returns four artifacts to the trunk via its
`canon-ledger.md`. Books connect through **the team, the emptying ledger, and the
walks** — the cycle runs a continuous cast (a first for the series) while every book
stands alone behind one member's eyes. Every book reads cold, from page one.
