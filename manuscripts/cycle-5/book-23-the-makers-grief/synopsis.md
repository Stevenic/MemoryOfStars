# The Makers' Grief — Synopsis

**Spoiler-complete.** Keep in sync with the [series bible](../../../series-bible/) and the
[canon ledger](canon-ledger.md).

Seven years on, one stratum refuses classification: it reads as two ages at once. Pim —
the Restorers' joiner, allergic to false wholes — listens at the cold face and hears the
join like a beat-frequency: wrong, and beautiful. Two strata were mis-compressed into one
ages ago — a builder's last morning welded to a first-rise mother's; un-fusing would
spend both, and leaving them fused preserves a lie. Hask moves to un-join blind; Kova,
carrying the only benign fusion alive, counters. The vote: walk it fused, once.

The impossible morning, walked raw: a farewell in an other grammar braided with a lullaby
in an ancestral human one, each answering the other across eight hundred thousand years.
Wrong. Right. Both. Pim surfaces shaking, unable to say which. Yent's tribunal suit seeks
an injunction against walking any contested stratum, quoting Hask's own logged dissents;
the ruling under its shadow: walk each thread to its source before any un-join. The
joiner will walk both.

The mother's thread runs to the golden age's last season — one household's ordinary
courage as the thinning came, the lullaby sung against the quiet: the first rise's end
witnessed at kitchen scale. Pim carries it up weeping in someone else's cadence. Deep in
the builder's thread the weld *pulls* — Pim, seconds from fusion, saved by the wrong-note
sense itself — and what the joiner walks out of is **the pouring**: the makers' rite of
dissolution, selves divided outward into ground and root and cold. Flight in some;
consent in some; both true, forever. They did not leave. They became the floor. And at
the pouring's edge one figure stays unpoured — steadying the most frightened of the
pourers the way you hand people down into a boat, then remaining to take up the tending
of the taking, so the collection would have a shepherd. Witnessed wordlessly, named
never.

The verdict is argued in work: the fusion was no accident — the threads were *filed
together*; the store has a curator's fingerprints, one fingerprint and no hand.
Un-joining would unmake an act of meaning, so Pim re-joins the walked threads as a
**chord** — the practice's first deliberate fusion, housed plural and holed, because *a
fused coherent record is the most collectable object there is*. Hask dissents formally
and signs the page anyway; the injunction narrows to nothing. And the joiner pays: the
wrong-note sense is permanently bent — Pim can never again hear a single grief singly.

The team stands on ordinary ground and cannot feel it as ordinary again; the vote for the
last two dark strata passes in silence. On the transcript's final page, farewell and
lullaby stand on one staff, with a marginal note in Pim's hand: *some things are only
true together.* Filed. The store, behind them, one era colder.
