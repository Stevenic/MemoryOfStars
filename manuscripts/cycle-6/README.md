# Cycle 6 — *The Memory of Stars*

Books 26–30. The title cycle: **the culmination**. The failing keeper's triage goes
erratic, the armor the reach built across twenty-five books begins to fail with it —
and the answer, at last, is not a fight. It is a **relief**: the watch taken up by
everyone, so that no single steward may ever again decide what a civilization
forgets.

This cycle alone runs **serial momentum** — Books 26–30 as one continuous movement,
the deliberate exception to the series' no-continuations rule. Each book still
stands behind one POV with a complete arc; the **watch-roll** (a count that goes
*up* — the final cycle's artifact) does the orienting on every first page. The cast
is the **committee of traditions**: a courier, a carrier, a listener, a
cartographer, a restorer — five seats, no chosen one, each indispensable, none
sufficient.

## Central questions

1. **The Gathering** — Who answers when everyone is called?
2. **The Last Archive** — What does the keeper owe the kept, at the end?
3. **The Unmaking** — What do you keep when home is being taken?
4. **What the Stars Remember** — What can a civilization do on purpose that it learned by accident?
5. **The Memory of Stars** — *What must a civilization remember if it hopes to survive?* — the series' own question, answered in action.

## Why this sequence works

The last cycle converges, loses, turns, and relieves:

> **the gathering → the stand → the Scouring → the great wearing → the relief**

The counterparty (the Regency — one warden versus everyone's watch) is present from
book one; the losses are real and stay lost; the climax is an act, not an answer —
and one mystery is kept forever, on the page, by choice. The series ends where it
began: a kitchen in Orin, bread and thyme, a child asking for the story — which is
now told the way this civilization keeps everything: plural, light, drifting a
little in the telling. That is how it survives.

## Shared-canon discipline

Each book returns four artifacts to the trunk via its `canon-ledger.md`. The cycle
returns to lit rooms (Valen, the vault, Orin, the threshold) and shows almost
nothing new — the last new place the series shows is small, and it is home.
