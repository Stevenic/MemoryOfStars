# The Gathering — Outline

**Memory of Stars — Novella #26** · **Target length:** ~31,800 words · **Structure:**
**No prologue — cold open.** 10 chapters in four movements (The Summons · The Roads ·
The Rival Table · The Compact) + Epilogue — chosen: romps open cold, and the final
cycle opens mid-route, already carrying mail.

- **Facet:** memory as **convergence**.
- **Central question:** *Who answers when everyone is called?*
- **Register — the cycle's romp (FIRM, confirmed at charter):** reunion warmth —
  the pleasure of threads meeting; twenty-five books of survivors, descendants,
  and institutions arriving at one table. The series' one bright book before the
  price. The bill lands in the fourth movement and stays paid.
- **The book's job:** open the title cycle — and open it under **serial momentum**
  (confirmed at charter: Books 26–30 run as one continuous movement, the sanctioned
  exception to the no-continuations rule; each book still stands behind one POV
  with a complete arc, and each opens on its own ground). The Testament's charge
  (*what we owe now is the ending*) is answered by two gatherings at once — the
  web's, and the Regency's — because the crisis that summons both is the series'
  worst news: **the armor has started to fail.**
- **POV:** Gale — thirty-three, a Wayfinders' Guild route-holder of the old school:
  the courier entrusted with carrying the Convening summons across the reach. Her
  tic, and it is the book's engine: she has never once failed to deliver — and
  this route's manifest keeps growing as she rides. **Voice note (the romp lives
  here):** Gale's register is courier's wit — complaint-as-love, dry marginalia
  in the manifest, the professional's running commentary on everyone's luggage.
  Draft her funny or the cycle's one bright book isn't.
- **The spine in one line:** a courier carrying the summons to the last convening
  gathers, road by road, the five traditions and their descendants — racing a
  rival convention that answers the same crisis with one throne — while the news
  that summoned everyone gets worse behind her: the failing keeper's triage has
  gone erratic, and for the first time in the series, something *plural-held* has
  been collected.

## The threads
- **The terminal failure (the cycle's engine, lit on page one):** the Custodian's
  decline (← Cycle 4, witnessed; ← Cycle 5, understood) has entered its last
  phase: the triage errs. A **relay-held roll was collected entire** — the first
  time in recorded history the armor was simply not honored. The rules the reach
  built its survival on are failing *with* the keeper. Relief is no longer
  philosophy. It is urgent care.
- **Two gatherings:** the web's Convening (the five traditions, called by the
  Testament's heirs) and **the Regency** — Chancellor Bode's coalition, decent
  and terrified, answering the same failure with the old good idea at final
  scale: *the watch must have a warden — one, accountable, ours.* The Mule's
  argument, matured into politics; the counterparty present from book one, per
  the series' hardest-learned lesson.
- **The roads as reunion:** Gale's route threads the lit map — Farrow's holders,
  Tolm's sextons, Meret's wardens, the Marigold's line, the motherhouse, the
  institute — the pleasure of threads meeting, carried at romp speed, each stop
  one scene and one passenger more.
- **The watch-roll (the cycle's artifact, debuting):** the Convening's roll of
  volunteers — a count that goes **up**. After twenty-five books of ledgers,
  dossiers, and decrements, the final cycle's document grows: every signature a
  person volunteering for a watch not yet defined. The counts differ world to
  world, and it does not matter. That is the point.
- **Chapter-Two Rule:** chapter 2 is the summons' reason, unsealed on the road —
  the collected relay-held roll, named, dated, verified thrice. The armor failed.
  Gale reads it twice and rides faster.

## Characters in play
- **Gale** — the courier. Never failed a delivery; the book asks what that means
  when the delivery is *everyone*.
- **Zel** — a carrier of Odri's practice, first passenger: dreams in two species,
  speaks for the carried. *(POV of Book 29.)*
- **Ketra** — a Listener, holder of a Reading emphasis. *(POV of Book 27.)*
- **Idra** — a Cartographer, keeper of the corrected preface. *(POV of Book 28.)*
- **Alba** — a Restorer, Amel's student, keeper of a Testament hole. *(POV of
  Book 30.)*
- **Chancellor Bode** — the Regency's founder: a decent man who watched the
  armor fail and concluded, reasonably, that failure needs a government. Wrong
  the way the series' best opponents are wrong — by one clause.
- **The elders** — cameo lattice, one scene each, never a reunion tour: Isla Ash
  (gray, claimless, amused), Advocate Yent (retired, unretained, helpful at
  last), Ondine — provost no longer (← Book 25) — the first to sign the watch-roll.

## The Writer's Test (must pass)
- [ ] New truth — the armor can fail: plural-held things collected as the keeper's
      triage goes erratic; the relief becomes urgent care. *(Ch 2, 7.)*
- [ ] A mystery left — whether the failures were the first or the first *noticed*;
      the door's location; what relief even means. *(Ch 2, 10.)*
- [ ] A character permanently changed — Gale: the courier who never failed learns
      what delivery means when the parcel is everyone; she joins the watch she
      carried. *(Ch 10.)*
- [ ] Civilization changed — the Compact exists; the watch-roll exists; the
      Regency exists. The reach has chosen its two answers. *(Ch 8–9.)*
- [ ] Larger universe — the erratic phase; the door implied; the last act begun.
      *(Ch 7, 10.)*

## Laws & mystery discipline
- **Serial momentum (charter-confirmed, scoped):** 26→30 run continuous; this book
  ends mid-motion *by design* — but its own arc (Gale's delivery) is complete, and
  Book 27 opens on its own ground with the watch-roll as the boarding artifact.
  No "previously-on" ever; the artifact does the orienting.
- **Romp discipline:** the warmth is real and the reader gets it whole; the bill
  (the Regency's size; the second failure; the race) lands in movement IV and is
  not softened. Nobody's reunion is spent as setup.
- **Counterparty discipline:** Bode is decent, present from Ch 5, and *winning* —
  the Regency out-gathers the Compact. The final cycle's opposition is never
  offstage again.
- **Canon-spend guard:** zero. The erratic failure is event, not explanation; the
  door is sought, not shown; the Custodian appears only as consequence.
- **No chosen one:** the committee is five seats, elected by five traditions;
  Gale assembles it because carrying is her job. Law 6, opening the last act.
- **Cast fence (at ceiling):** ten named is the maximum — Ch 4's three-stop
  montage adds *no* further named characters; the stops stay offices, not names.
- **Two doors, one guard:** the letters' margin *triangulates* the threshold —
  navigation data only. The far door (Book 1's tower; the watchers; D3: mail
  only) is never the destination and never conflated with the dead address.

- **Travel texture (tech-bible §8):** Gale's route is stops, tolls, and
  depot-light — the summons travels at the speed of the roads, and the manifest
  is a map of what the roads allow. No drive-words; the page shows the riding.
