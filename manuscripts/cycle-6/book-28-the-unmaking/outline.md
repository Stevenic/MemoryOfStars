# The Unmaking — Outline

**Memory of Stars — Novella #28** · **Target length:** ~31,400 words · **Structure:**
Prologue + 3 acts × 3 chapters + Epilogue — **the founding shape, on the founding
ground:** the Scouring runs on Book 1's own grid, because this book happens where the
series was born and owes it the family architecture.

- **Facet:** memory as **loss**.
- **Central question:** *What do you keep when home is being taken?*
- **The book's job — THE SCOURING (constitutional, owed since v0.4):** before the
  ending, the Erasure touches **Orin itself**. Gorged on the Witness and failing
  faster, the collector's erratic arc crosses the protected first place — the Gate,
  the Hall, the mountain, the town the reader has loved since page one. The stakes
  come home, witnessed and felt, so the resolution can earn its grace. This is the
  series' darkest book, and it is set in its warmest rooms.
- **POV:** Idra — thirty-eight, Cartographer, keeper of the corrected preface; the
  committee's witness-of-record. Her tic, the institute's discipline worn personal:
  she maps *while it happens* — grief deferred into fieldwork — and this book is
  the ground where the method finally cannot hold the hand still.
- **The spine in one line:** the committee races the erratic collection to Orin —
  deploying everything twenty-seven books built on the ground where the first book
  happened — and half of it holds and half of it fails, and the cartographer whose
  job is the losses must map the town the series comes from while it is being
  taken, and find in the wreckage the one proof that decides the ending.

## The threads
- **The Scouring, in kind (per the standing guard):** not a record blanked
  off-site — Orin *touched*, on-page, felt: the Gate falling silent; the Hall's
  rolls blanking faster than relay teams copy; the mountain's substructure — the
  series' first lit room — going cold the way the Wells went cold. Witnessed by
  the reader's own memory of Book 1, which is the point and the price.
- **The armor, half-holding:** everything deploys — relay-held rolls, the Night
  of Strangers, incompleteness surgery, the responders' fifth edition — and under
  erratic triage it *half*-works: what is worn holds mostly; what is assumed
  fails terribly. The book's grief is an audit; its horror is randomness.
- **The plaque (the series' oldest object, tested):** the SAEL plaque — singular,
  coherent, beloved: by every rule, bait. The town's choice about it is the
  book's moral center — and the outcome is the cycle's proof: **the plaque is
  taken; the name holds** — carried in mouths since Book 2 made it a case,
  plural past any collector's reach. Grief and vindication in a single beat.
- **The proof (the turn's fuel, → Book 29):** Idra's live survey of the Scouring
  produces the cycle's decisive data: the erratic failures correlate not with
  the armor's *design* but with its *practice* — worn daily, it held even here;
  assumed, it failed even here. What survives is not the plural in principle.
  It is the plural **in use**. The thesis, priced in the town that taught it.
- **Chapter-Two Rule:** chapter 2 is the Gate falling silent — mid-festival, in
  front of the whole town, on the day of the Pilgrimage rite the reader knows by
  heart.

## Characters in play
- **Idra** — the cartographer. Maps while it happens; the book takes the method
  to its limit and then takes the pencil out of her hand.
- **The town** — Orin, five hundred years on: Mara's kitchen kept by her line's
  heirs (bread, thyme, the two bowls, unchanged); the Hall's keepers; the
  festival calendar the reader learned in Book 1, running as the dark arrives.
- **Gale, Zel, Ketra, Alba** — the committee deployed: routes, carriage,
  deposition, restoration — each tradition working its craft on the first
  ground, each getting one defining beat, none crowding the POV.
- **Chancellor Bode** — absent by choice: the Regency wards its own worlds
  instead. The empty chair where help could have stood is his book-28 argument,
  and the reach notices.
- **The keeper's keeper** — the collector, erratically at work: never spoken
  to, never speaking; a failing hand taking wrong things gently. Pitied and
  feared at once, on the ground it has come to.

## The Writer's Test (must pass)
- [ ] New truth — the Scouring: even the first place is not exempt; the decisive
      correlation (worn holds; assumed fails) — the practice is the armor.
      *(Ch 2, 8.)*
- [ ] A mystery left — the Gate's nature, glimpsed only as it is lost (enlarged,
      never explained); where the taken go, kept to the last. *(Ch 6.)*
- [ ] A character permanently changed — Idra: the mapper whose method broke at
      home; the completed map wept over is her testimony. *(Ch 8.)*
- [ ] Civilization changed — Orin scoured and standing; the proof that decides
      the relief's design; the Regency's empty chair, noticed reach-wide.
      *(Ch 7–9.)*
- [ ] Larger universe — the collector has taken a limb of its own network; the
      failure is accelerating toward an end nobody has named. *(Ch 6 → 29.)*

## Laws & mystery discipline
- **The Scouring guard, discharged in kind:** witnessed, felt, at Orin — this is
  the scheduled beat, different in kind from Book 24's off-site blank exactly as
  the fences required. Elias's blank is *not* referenced or hinged on; the
  Scouring's griefs are its own.
- **Serial momentum (scoped):** own POV, complete arc, opens on its own ground
  (the festival), the watch-roll orienting from the prologue's first page —
  Orin's blank column *is* the orientation.
- **No cheap resurrection:** the plaque stays taken; the Gate stays silent; the
  mountain stays cold. What holds is what was practiced — nothing returns.
- **Applicability guard:** Orin's loss renders in civic and ritual terms (a
  silent gate, an amended calendar, a kept kitchen); no draft maps it onto any
  single real-world loss; the grieving stays specific — a plaque, a rite, a
  loaf.
- **Book 1 discipline:** every echo is Book 1's own furniture (the festival, the
  gesture, the plaque, the epilogue title); the Gate's midpoint glimpse must
  *enlarge* its mystery, never explain it — the intentionally-undefined list
  survives the Scouring intact.
- **No chosen one:** the town saves itself with practiced customs; the committee
  assists; nobody is elected by the mountain. Law 6, at home.
