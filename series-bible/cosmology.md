# Cosmology & the Rules of the Universe

The mechanics of the universe — the part readers must never catch cheating. The binding
constraints are the [Seven Laws](seven-laws.md); this file is where we pin down specifics
**only as stories demand them**, and never before.

> **Design stance:** we define _constraints_, not _technology_ (see [seven-laws.md](seven-laws.md)).
> Memory is the underlying **force** that shapes the universe — not a recurring "memory loss"
> plot. See [foundation.md → Memory as Force](foundation.md#design-decision--memory-as-force-not-plot-device).

## Memory as the central mechanic

Per [Law 2 — Memory Outlives Flesh](seven-laws.md#law-2--memory-outlives-flesh): everything
leaves memories behind — biological, digital, cultural, architectural. Nothing truly
disappears; the question is whether anyone can still _interpret_ what remains. Archaeology,
religion, politics, and identity all flow from this.

_As the series establishes how memory is stored, inherited, corrupted, and recovered in
specific cases, record those specifics here so they stay consistent._

## Deliberately undefined

These stay open until a story genuinely needs the answer — see the full list in
[seven-laws.md → Things That Are Intentionally Undefined](seven-laws.md#things-that-are-intentionally-undefined)
(FTL, the builders of the oldest megastructures, the nature of the "gods", whether Earth
still lives, and more).

**When a story answers one, it's a canon event** — record the answer in
[`canon-and-continuity.md`](canon-and-continuity.md) and update this file.

## Established specifics

_Constraints only — mechanisms stay undefined on the page until their scheduled cycle
(the reveal ladder lives in `planning/`)._

| Topic | What's now canon | Set by |
|-------|------------------|--------|
| Travel | Ships are **sublight, always** — no FTL matter. Crossings between stars cost years to decades; a new colony is a generation's bet (Meret → Sowen: ~9 ly, ~20 years). | Author (firm, planning tech-bible); Books 04/07 |
| Communication | Near-instant messaging exists **only along routes someone already physically traveled**; extending the network means a slow ferry that can be lost. Dark or dead segments carry signal at **light speed** — the Signal's 35-year crawl. | Books 02/03; author design |
| Presence vs. travel | To be linked is to share a present; a severed world falls out of the shared *now*. Silence and distance are the same phenomenon here. | Books 06/09/10 (dramatized); ratified 2026-08 |
| Reckoning | Time: **the Toll** (human zero — the First Bell, Gault; CR 0 = Toll 7,214). Position: **the Bell-frame** (x/y/z, years of light; origin inherited from the old network — see the atlas). Local calendars stand, per Law 1. | Ratified 2026-08; `timeline.md` + `atlas.md` |
| Galactic frame | A spiral galaxy like ours: two great arms + two minor, flat rotation (~213-My galactic year at the pocket). Bands shear ~30 ly per My per 1,000 ly of radius — the sky forgets its own arrangements on My–Gy scales. The walked pocket is a **co-moving stellar association**: internal drift only ~15 ly/My, so the local map holds and the Bell-frame rides the family. Over the series' five centuries, drift is negligible (< 0.01 ly). | Ratified 2026-08; model `scripts/gen-galaxy.rb` |
| The census | **~300 billion stars.** The network has ever touched on the order of 10⁵ systems; **11,406 answer today** (the almanac, and falling — Cycle 4 tracks the decrements). The knowable is a cobweb in a cathedral: about **one star in three million**, on one spur of one arm. Everything outside it is night — visible by light, unreachable by conversation. | Ratified 2026-08 |
| Civilizations | **About a dozen attested, ever** — humanity, the Selt, the makers, the Choir, the prior human rise, Aulis's nine buried rises, the caretaker remnants — and **two or three extant**. A Drake-style expectation, given how generously life demonstrably arises here (nine rises on one ordinary world), runs to *tens of thousands concurrent*. The shortfall is the point, twice over: **"known" means *in the conversation*** — a civilization is known only if the network reaches it or remembers it — **and silence has two causes, severed or collected, indistinguishable from inside.** The Erasure is this universe's answer to the Fermi paradox. One signal in the whole series ever arrives without a relay (01 → 30). | Ratified 2026-08 |
