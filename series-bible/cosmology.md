# Cosmology & the Rules of the Universe

The mechanics of the universe — the part readers must never catch cheating. The binding
constraints are the [Seven Laws](seven-laws.md); this file is where we pin down specifics
**only as stories demand them**, and never before.

> **Design stance:** we define _constraints_, not _technology_ (see [seven-laws.md](seven-laws.md)).
> Memory is the underlying **force** that shapes the universe — not a recurring "memory loss"
> plot. See [foundation.md → Memory as Force](foundation.md#design-decision--memory-as-force-not-plot-device).

## Memory as the central mechanic

Per [Law 2 — Memory Outlives Flesh](seven-laws.md#law-2--memory-outlives-flesh): everything
leaves memories behind — biological, digital, cultural, architectural. Nothing truly
disappears; the question is whether anyone can still _interpret_ what remains. Archaeology,
religion, politics, and identity all flow from this.

_As the series establishes how memory is stored, inherited, corrupted, and recovered in
specific cases, record those specifics here so they stay consistent._

## Deliberately undefined

These stay open until a story genuinely needs the answer — see the full list in
[seven-laws.md → Things That Are Intentionally Undefined](seven-laws.md#things-that-are-intentionally-undefined)
(FTL, the builders of the oldest megastructures, the nature of the "gods", whether Earth
still lives, and more).

**When a story answers one, it's a canon event** — record the answer in
[`canon-and-continuity.md`](canon-and-continuity.md) and update this file.
