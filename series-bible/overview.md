# Overview

Orientation for the series. The authoritative identity of the universe lives in
[`foundation.md`](foundation.md) and [`seven-laws.md`](seven-laws.md) — this file
doesn't restate them; it maps how the **30 novellas** sit inside them.

## In one breath

*Memory of Stars* is a mythic science fiction universe about the rise, flourishing, and
decline of civilizations across millions of years. It asks: **What must a civilization
remember if it hopes to survive?** Its organizing metaphor is that **civilization is a
conversation across generations** — and every story tests whether that conversation is
preserved, distorted, interrupted, or rediscovered.

## The 30-book arc

> The novellas are the **backstory foundation** for the wider universe (future books and a
> potential game). They're organized as **6 cycles of 5**, and need not be told in
> chronological order. Each book must pass [The Writer's Test](seven-laws.md#the-writers-test)
> and explore a facet of memory-as-force rather than repeating "memory loss" as a plot.

## What the reader should feel

Ancient and optimistic at once; awe before fear; wonder before spectacle. Even at its
darkest, the belief that people can rebuild what was lost. (Full tone spec:
[`foundation.md` → Tone](foundation.md#tone).)
