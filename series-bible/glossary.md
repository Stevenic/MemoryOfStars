# Glossary

Canonical spellings and definitions for coined terms, names, and in-world vocabulary.
Add every new term the first time it appears in a manuscript. *(Backfilled 2026-08 from
the Book 1 draft and Book 2's drafted Act I; outline-stage terms enter via `canonize`
when their books draft.)*

> _Keep alphabetical. One line each; link to a fuller bible entry when one exists._

**Amarin** — a role, not a name: the responsibility assembled from fragments, taken up
by whoever answers; "the one who carries what cannot safely belong to anyone."
_(first appears: book 01)_

**The Archive** — Orin's name for what waits at the Gate: the presence that receives
the Pilgrimage and answers, when it answers, in sevens. _(first appears: book 01)_

**Book of Relationships** — the Hall of Names' great record: not what happened, but
who remembers it with whom; 211 volumes and counting. _(first appears: book 01;
formalized book 02)_

**The erasure** *(always lowercase on the page)* — Orin's folk word for the force that
unmakes memory; spoken of obliquely, never explained. _(first appears: book 01)_

**The farewell** — *"Come home carrying more people than yourself,"* with its
two-finger gesture; Orin's parting blessing. Motif: reproduce verbatim, never
paraphrase. **One deliberate exception:** Book 22's epilogue renders the phrase's
deep-past ancestor *close-not-verbatim, by design*, so Book 25 can close the gap —
do not "correct" that plant (see both outlines). _(first appears: book 01)_

**Festival of Last Things** — Orin's leave-taking feast on the eve of a Pilgrimage;
borrowed possessions, returned changed. _(first appears: book 01)_

**The Gate** — the structure above Orin through which Pilgrims pass; whether the
mountain is a place, a machine, or something else is deliberately open.
_(first appears: book 01)_

**Hall of Names** — Orin's archive-institution; keeper of the Book of Relationships;
by Book 2, one node of something larger. _(first appears: book 01)_

**The Pilgrimage** — Orin's rite: the chosen climb to the Gate to receive and return
the town's memories; CR 0 is dated from Lyra Ash's. _(first appears: book 01)_

**Resonance Lens** — the instrument beneath Valen's oldest archive that shows
*relationships*, not truth; "found, not made." _(first appears: book 02)_

**SAEL** — the plaque with one name, hung in Orin: the only place her name is written;
remembered by more than one person, which is enough. _(first appears: book 01)_

**The Society** — Valen's historical society; institutional patron of the
one-authoritative-record ambition. _(first appears: book 02)_
