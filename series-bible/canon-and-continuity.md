# Canon & Continuity

## The canon hierarchy

When two sources disagree, the higher one wins:

1. **[foundation.md](foundation.md) + [seven-laws.md](seven-laws.md)** — the constitution. Nothing overrides these.
2. **Published novellas.**
3. **Completed drafts.**
4. **The rest of this series bible** (overview, cosmology, timeline, entries).
5. **Notes and outlines.**

## The acceptance test

No novella is "done" until it passes **[The Writer's Test](seven-laws.md#the-writers-test)**:

- [ ] Teaches the reader one new truth about the universe.
- [ ] Leaves one important mystery unsolved.
- [ ] Permanently changes at least one character.
- [ ] Changes civilization itself, even a little.
- [ ] Makes the universe feel larger at the end than at the start.

## Continuity rules

- Every recurring detail (names, dates, powers, geography, memory-mechanics) must match the bible.
- When you change canon, update the bible **and** any affected synopses in the same pass.
- Honor the [Seven Laws](seven-laws.md) — a story that breaks one needs rewriting, not a retcon.
