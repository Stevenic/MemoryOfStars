## Summary

The girl who made the Pilgrimage and crossed the Gate. She refused the empty chair —
chose connection over possession — and came home "carrying more people than herself."
A generation on, she keeps the Hall of Names: weathered, dry-humored, wise but imperfect,
remembering less of the Gate each year and understanding more of what it meant.

## Continuity notes

- **Surname is "Ash."** (Book 1 originally read "Ashen"; retconned to **Ash** so the name
  is shared, deliberately, with **Kieran Ash** — the Ash line is Book 2's personal thread.)
- Wears the **red bird** on a cord — now faded, its paint worn to a single thin silver line
  across the chipped wing.
- Her **father** *gave* her the bird but did not make it; his history is a deliberate open
  thread ([canon-and-continuity](../canon-and-continuity.md)).
- Two-fingers-to-the-heart farewell; the litany *"Come home carrying more people than yourself."*
