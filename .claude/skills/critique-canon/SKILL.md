# Critique-canon — what must be true

One lens: **the text against the bible.** You critique; you do not rewrite — findings go
to `critiques/canon.md` (format: the findings contract in the `critique` skill) for the
`edit` skill to apply.

## Load first

`series-bible/foundation.md` · `seven-laws.md` (Laws, the intentionally-undefined list) ·
`cosmology.md` · `timeline.md` · `glossary.md` · `canon-and-continuity.md` · relevant
`characters/` `locations/` `factions/` entries · `writing-guide/naming-conventions.md`
(+ don't-reuse list) · the book's `canon-ledger.md` and `synopsis.md`.

## The rubric

- **Constitution:** any contradiction of `foundation.md`/`seven-laws.md` outranks everything.
- **The Seven Laws — check each explicitly:**
  1. *Distance Is Real* — free/instant travel? real-time galactic anything?
  2. *Memory Outlives Flesh* — memory-mechanics consistent with established rules?
  3. *Intelligence Is Not Life* — human exceptionalism? evil-AI cliché?
  4. *Technology Changes Culture* — an important tech with no social consequence?
  5. *Every Civilization Forgets Something* — memory treated as complete/perfect?
  6. *There Are No Chosen Ones* — prophecy, destiny, "the one"? (*"The Gate does not
     choose people. It encounters them."*)
  7. *Wonder Comes First* — one earned awe beat present? (shared with critique-story.)
- **Intentionally undefined:** does the text *answer* a deliberately-open question (FTL,
  the gods, is Earth alive, who built the megastructures…)? That's a universe-level
  canon event — Blocker unless logged in `canon-and-continuity.md` with author sign-off.
- **Established facts:** names, ages, places, dates, object-rules match bible + ledger +
  glossary. (Book 1 anchors: the bird *passed through Sael*; her father *gave but did
  not make* it; **Amarin is a role**; two-fingers-to-heart; the bells past six.)
- **Motifs & litany verbatim:** the farewell line, the gesture, established refrains —
  flag *any* drift, quote both versions.
- **Internal continuity:** counts, timelines, who-knows-what within the text itself.
- **Naming:** new coinages fit `naming-conventions.md` phonetics and registers; no
  collisions with the don't-reuse list; new terms flagged for the glossary
  (`canonize` files them).
- **Spoiler firewall:** nothing from `planning/` or later books leaks onto the page
  beyond what a published book has revealed.

Do not invent lore to judge against — if the bible is silent, write it as an open
question. Every finding: verbatim anchor quote + the specific rule.
