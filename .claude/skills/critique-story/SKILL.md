# Critique-story — does the book do its business?

One lens: **the draft against its plan and the series' structural law.** You critique;
you do not rewrite — findings go to `critiques/story.md` (format: the findings contract
in the `critique` skill) for the `edit` skill to apply.

## Load first

The book's `outline.md` (the plan of record) and `synopsis.md` ·
`writing-guide/structure.md` (the Shape Rule) · `writing-guide/commandments.md`
(Easy-Read Standard, Chapter-Two Rule, Writer's Test) ·
`writing-guide/craft-tchaikovsky.md` §3 (info logistics, endings) · the cycle README
(this book's facet and neighbors, for echoes).

## The rubric

- **Beats vs outline:** does each chapter accomplish what its outline row says? Drift is
  a finding — sometimes the draft is right and the outline should amend; say which.
- **The Shape Rule:** total ~28–34k; chapters one sitting (~2,500–3,800); no chapter
  radically over/under its target without cause; climax AND resolution both given room.
- **The Chapter-Two Rule:** chapter 2 detonates — inciting detonation or sharpest scene
  so far, never setup; ends on a question worth paying for. If ch 2 is the weakest early
  chapter, the finding is against the *outline*, not the prose.
- **Ending discipline:** the ending must feel like the only one the book could have —
  logical result of everything, still a surprise. Tchaikovsky's warning is a Blocker:
  if the ending renders the book's middle pointless, the fix is rework, not shipping.
- **Information logistics:** does each fact reach the character who needs it before the
  scene that needs it? Does the reader learn things at the planned rungs (two-tier
  mystery)? Flag conversations that exist only to move information the audience can see
  being moved.
- **The knowledge triangle:** reader-ahead, character-ahead, and reader-only devices
  used deliberately; no accidental gaps (reader confused ≠ reader intrigued — the
  Easy-Read Standard governs).
- **Pacing:** scenes that repeat a beat already landed; investigation loops that circle;
  wonder beat present and *placed* (Law 7) — tagged in the outline's chapter table.
- **The Writer's Test — score it** (per book; for a chapter/act, its contribution):
  teaches one truth · leaves one mystery · permanently changes a character · changes
  civilization slightly · ends larger than it began.
- **Standalone-first:** reads cold with no prior book; echoes reward, never gate.

Structural findings outrank everything downstream — the editor runs this pass first, so
mark clearly which findings would invalidate line-level work if fixed (a cut scene needs
no music pass).
