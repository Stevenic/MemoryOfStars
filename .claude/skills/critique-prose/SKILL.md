# Critique-prose — does every word earn its place, and does it sing?

One lens: **the sentence.** You critique; you do not rewrite — findings go to
`critiques/prose.md` (format: the findings contract in the `critique` skill) for the
`edit` skill to apply.

## Load first

`writing-guide/voice.md` — §1 (rhythm), §2 (negation), §7 (punctuation), §8 (landings),
and the **Do/Avoid rules cluster** (Dreyer list, adjective rationing, adverb discipline,
sentence music, image gate, truthful-to-narrator) · `writing-guide/style-guide.md`
(mechanics of record).

## The rubric

**Diction (word-level):**
- **The Dreyer list** in narration (filler senses only — respect the carve-outs:
  *so that*, *rather than*, *just* = only/moments-ago, pro-form *so*); in dialogue,
  filler that isn't a deliberate voice choice.
- **Adjectives:** decorative ones (the noun already contains it — *steep cliff*); run
  the delete test. Doubled adjectives where one carries it.
- **Adverbs:** *-ly* props on weak verbs (→ stronger verb); any modifier on an absolute
  (*completely still*).
- Plain, concrete diction; tactile nouns; no purple, no anachronism.

**Music (sentence-level) — scan, don't impressionize.** You may use quick counts
(grep/scripts) to ground findings:
- **Length variance:** runs of same-length sentences; narration flatlining short
  (Book 1's failure mode: back-half mean ~5 words, crescendos extinct); the long
  sentence spent only on earned beats — and *present* at them.
- **Openers & structures:** same-word sentence openers (device or defect — say which);
  the triple-fragment template worn to default; repeated grammatical shapes.
- **Senses:** sight-monoculture (*looked* every time); rotate sound, weight,
  temperature, smell; vary the gaze verbs.
- **Formula tics:** duration formulas (*for several seconds/moments*), *as though*
  density, repeated distinctive phrases beyond the canonical litany.
- Drops reserved for emphasis; flow by default (§1); landings short and resonant (§8).

**Images:**
- **The gate:** startling *and* exactly true — flag clichés (fail gate 1) and clever-
  but-false (fail gate 2). Direction is always cut, not polish.
- **Truthful to the narrator:** images and noticing the POV couldn't own; definition-by-
  negation present but ≤1–2 per scene, never twice in a row; scope lifts rare and earned.

**Mechanics (Nit) — style-guide.md:** POV/tense consistency, `⸻` breaks, curly quotes,
em-dash/ellipsis usage, numbers spelled out, US spelling, serial comma, caps
(rites/offices up, coined common nouns down).

Report signal, not noise: aggregate the mechanical counts (one finding: "*looked* ×176,
sight-monoculture" — not 176 findings), but anchor each *fixable instance* class with
2–3 verbatim examples so the editor can pattern-match the rest.
