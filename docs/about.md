**The Memory of Stars** is a mythic science fiction universe about the rise, flourishing,
and decline of civilizations across millions of years.

It is not a story about spaceships. It is not a story about artificial intelligence. It
is not a story about war. It is a story about **memory** — because every civilization is
built on what it chooses to remember, what it chooses to forget, and what it mistakes for
truth.

Humanity has spread across thousands of star systems. Some colonies are decades old;
others have endured for hundreds of thousands of years, and many have forgotten Earth
entirely. Ancient machines and intelligences bind these worlds together — so old that no
one remembers who built them. To some they are tools. To others, gods.

This universe asks one question:

> **What must a civilization remember if it hopes to survive?**

## What to expect

Wonder before spectacle. Curiosity before action. No chosen ones and no ultimate evil —
just people, curious and stubborn enough to ask the questions everyone else stopped asking
centuries ago. Every story reveals one new truth about the cosmos and opens one new
mystery, so the universe always feels larger than when you began.

## The shape of it

The foundation is **thirty novellas** — the events, people, and turning points the wider
series of books, and one day a game, are built upon. This site is where that universe
becomes public: the books as they're ready, the lore behind them, and news along the way.
