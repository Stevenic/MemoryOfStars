In the mountain town of Orin, every child must one day make the Pilgrimage: a rite in
which the young enter an ancient intelligence and live, for a while, inside the carefully
chosen memories of generations of the dead. It is how Orin turns its children into adults —
by giving them the past to carry.

Then Lyra finds a memory the Archive swears does not exist.

*The First Pilgrim* is the doorway into Memory of Stars — memory as an intimate, human
inheritance before it becomes infrastructure, faith, or power. It asks the first of the
universe's questions: **what must we inherit before we can call ourselves adults?**
