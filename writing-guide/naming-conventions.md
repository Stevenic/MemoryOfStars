# Naming Conventions

Consistent naming keeps the universe coherent. Record the sound and logic of names here,
and add every coined term to the [glossary](../series-bible/glossary.md).

## People

Short, worn, consonant-grounded, English-adjacent: one or two syllables, no apostrophes
(Lyra, Elias, Veya, Tilde, Mott, Nell, Kest, Tess, Isla, Gale, Zel, Alba). Surnames are
rare and load-bearing when they appear (the Ash line). Titles are offices, not honorifics
(Keeper, Bearer, Warden, Provost, Prime, Chancellor) — an office outlives its holder and
the series treats that as the point.

## Places

**Every world and star has a name — even the ones no page speaks** (doctrine ratified
2026-08; the [atlas gazetteer](../series-bible/atlas.md#gazetteer--every-star-and-world-named-doctrine-ratified-2026-08)
is the registry of record).

- **Worlds carry settler names:** short, homely, often plain words — Moss, Farrow,
  Fell, Quay, Marrow, Reed. The people who stay name a place the way they name a
  field.
- **Stars carry roll-names:** one hard syllable, coined by navigators — Beck,
  Torr, Voss, Loam, Gloam. *(Auros, the founding exception, predates the doctrine —
  the site's Archive named it first, and published names win.)* A star is a thing you steer by, not a thing you live on;
  its name lives in rolls and almanacs, which is why daily speech on the page almost
  never says one.
- **Cities and towns take the local register** (Orin, Valen, Meret, Carath, Tolm's
  temple-terraces, Lowen's Rest); rivers and features take plain words (the Sil, the
  Harrow valley).
- **Withholding is a page-level choice, not a registry gap.** Where namelessness is
  diegetic (the unroaded system **Lull**, which space was made to forget; **Scrive**,
  whose own order unwrote it; **Hearth**, only ever "the threshold" on the page), the
  registry holds the name and the page keeps its silence. An unspoken name is a
  reserve, not a debt.

## Factions & titles

Plural, descriptive, self-chosen: the Wayfinders' Guild, the Listeners, the
Cartographers of the End, the Restorers, the Unwritten, the Regency, the Compact.
No acronyms, no empire-speak. A faction's name states its practice.

## Coined terms

Plain compounds and worn phrases over invented vocabulary: route-memory, the patient
mail, the long shelf, relay-held, the great wearing, the watch-roll. Prefer a common
word given freight to a new word given a gloss. No apostrophe-coinage. Pluralize
normally.

Reserved (time & position, 2026-08): **the First Bell** (the original temporal
transmitter, on Gault), **the Toll** (its count — the institutional reckoning; Toll 0
= the Bell's first sounding; CR 0 = Toll 7,214), **the Bell-frame** (x/y/z in years
of light, origin at the Bell — see the atlas). Prose keeps saying "years"; the Toll
belongs to ledgers, rolls, manifests, and the mail.

## The don't-reuse list

Kept current so 30 books don't collide. Personal names in use: see each cycle's
outlines (POV and cast lists) — roughly a hundred names; grep before coining.
Worlds and stars now reserved by the gazetteer:

- **Worlds/systems:** Enara, Reed, Lull, Marrow, Gault, Quay, Fell, Aven, Hush,
  Scrive, Cairn, Vane, Holt, Mirren, Hearth (plus the established Orin, Valen, Meret,
  Farrow, Sowen, Carath, Aulis, Tolm, Penna, Moss, the Skerry, the Selt, Corvane,
  Dresk).
- **Stars:** Auros (and Auros B, *the Watcher* — Enara's red companion), Karst, Beck,
  Torr, Harl, Grell, Pike, Salt, Lorn, Voss, Cinder, Gloam, Crook, Murr, Blanch,
  Plume, Nock, Loam, Squall, Garn, Keld.
- **Minor bodies (planets, moons, belts — the systems registry):** settler names
  **repeat freely across systems** — isolation breeds convergence, and every reach
  world has its Kiln and its Char the way Earth has its Springfields. Uniqueness is
  required only *within* a system, and at the registry tier (worlds, stars, systems),
  which is unique galaxy-wide. Retired names (the rename record) are banned at every
  tier — three independent coiners reached for "Tarn"; none survived.
- **Precedence note (2026-08):** Enara/Auros were established first by the site's
  Archive (star chart + system file) and supersede the atlas's short-lived
  Alder/Sarn coinage — published names win. Renamed historically: see the rename
  record in [planning/the-long-mystery.md](../planning/the-long-mystery.md).
