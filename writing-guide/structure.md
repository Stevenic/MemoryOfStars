# Novella Structure — the series signature

**Version:** 1.0 · **Status:** Canonical (craft) · **Established by:** *The First Pilgrim* (Book 01).

Every Memory of Stars novella uses the **same structural shape**. The story, cast, setting,
and genre evolve wildly across the 30 books — the *cadence* does not. A familiar rhythm lets
readers focus on the evolving universe instead of relearning the pacing each time.

## The shape

```
Prologue
Act I    — Chapters 1 · 2 · 3
Act II   — Chapters 4 · 5 · 6
Act III  — Chapters 7 · 8 · 9
Epilogue
```

**Prologue + 9 chapters (3 acts × 3) + Epilogue = 11 sections.** Hold this shape unless a
story has a genuine reason not to — consistency of form is what frees variation of content.

## What each part does

- **Prologue** — an oblique cold-open, often a different POV or time; plants the central mystery and the tone. Usually **spare**. *(Bk 1: Elias at the Gate. Bk 2: the Observation of the Lens.)*
- **Act I (1–3)** — the ordinary world and the first cracks. Establish the cast, the book's **memory-facet**, and the inciting mystery; end on departure or commitment.
- **Act II (4–6)** — the journey/investigation deepens; the uncanny escalates; a midpoint reversal; approach the threshold.
- **Act III (7–9)** — the climax, its **permanent cost**, and the resolution. The facet's argument lands; at least one character is changed for good.
- **Epilogue** — aftermath and the **hook forward** — an *echo* toward what comes next, not a cliffhanger sequel.

## Word budget

- **Total: ~28–34k words.**
- **Per-chapter targets are flexible guides, not contracts.** Calibrate to how the series
  actually drafts (learned from Book 1):
  - full chapters land **~2,600–3,000**; big set-pieces run to **~4,000**; connective chapters **~2,000**; prologues run **spare** (Book 1's was ~420).
  - Budget the **climax and the resolution both generously**. Book 1's outline made the
    opposite mistake — a 4,000 climax and a 1,500 resolution — and drafted the reverse.

## Non-negotiables regardless of shape

- Every book must still pass **[The Writer's Test](../series-bible/seven-laws.md#the-writers-test)**.
- Scene breaks are a lone `⸻`; a chapter may run continuous (no breaks) when the prose is
  deliberately world-dissolving (Book 1, Ch. 8–9). See [`voice.md`](voice.md).
- Chapter headings: `## Prologue — Title`, `## Chapter N — Title`, `## Epilogue — Title`.
