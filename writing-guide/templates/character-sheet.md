# Character Sheet — quick reference

_A lightweight sheet for drafting. Promote recurring characters to a full entry in
[`series-bible/characters/`](../../series-bible/characters/)._

## Who they are

- **Name:** _..._ (short, sayable — see [naming-conventions](../naming-conventions.md); add to the book's `audio.yml` lexicon if pronunciation isn't obvious)
- **Wants (external goal):** _..._
- **Needs (internal truth):** _..._
- **Wound / backstory:** _..._
- **Flaw:** _..._
- **Arc in one line:** _..._

## How they talk (the conversation layer — [`voice.md`](../voice.md) §3)

- **Speech engine:** _everything they say is a version of ___ ._ (e.g. — Tomas: an
  inventory; Mara: tending something on the stove while watching you. Derived from what
  they are and what they want; when a line could belong to anyone, run it through the
  engine until it can't.)
- **Deflection:** _how they dodge a question they don't want to answer._ (Every speaker
  deflects differently — this, not tags, keeps untagged lines trackable.)
- **Voice in one line:** _diction, rhythm, register — words no one but them would use._

## How they move (the choreography layer — [`voice.md`](../voice.md) §3)

- **Gesture repertoire:** _2–3 signature micro-gestures, rationed like salt._ (e.g. —
  Tomas aligns and counts things; Mara's hands stay busy while her eyes don't; Elias
  goes still.) _Never the same gesture twice in a scene._
- **Tell:** _the one involuntary signal that shows what they won't say — spend rarely._
