# Voice & Craft

The craft layer around the sentence-level voice. Anchored to the
[foundation](../series-bible/foundation.md): **ancient and optimistic at once, awe before
fear, wonder before spectacle, curiosity before action.** Every story earns one moment of
"Wow" ([Law 7](../series-bible/seven-laws.md#law-7--wonder-comes-first)).

## The series voice

**See [`voice.md`](voice.md) for the full, example-driven voice specification** — flowing
paragraphs with the single-line drop reserved for emphasis, definition-by-negation, deadpan
dialogue with the beats attached, emotion carried by the body, the flatly-rendered uncanny,
and the canonical litany. This file covers the broader craft around that voice.

## Rhythm & sentence craft

Flow by default; drop for emphasis (voice.md §1). Group sentences that share a thought
into natural paragraphs and save the single-line drop for the few beats that earn it. Vary
any repeated-reply ladder (*"No." / "No." / "No."*) — at most one bare repetition, then a
body or a sharper variant. A page of identical short lines reads as a metronome, not a
voice. Sentence-length music, opener/structure variation, and the full prose-rules
cluster: voice.md Do/Avoid.

## Description & sensory grounding

The emotion lives in the body and the object, never in a named feeling (voice.md §6).
Ground every scene in a few tactile, workaday nouns (dough, winterleaf, a charcoal stylus,
a wet spoon) and let hands do the emotional work — a strap retied though it was already
tight says more than any labeled fear. Everything through the POV character's senses.

## Dialogue

Beats attached; never more than ~3 clipped exchanges without a body (voice.md §3). Each
character deflects differently — that difference, not tags, is what keeps untagged lines
trackable. Subtext through answering slightly off-axis; wit through literalism and
understatement. The conversation layer (speech engines, two agendas, the said-instead):
voice.md §3.

## Handling memory — rendering the central force *(developed 2026-08)*

Memory is the universe's organizing force, not a "memory-loss" plot device
([foundation](../series-bible/foundation.md#design-decision--memory-as-force-not-plot-device)).
On the page it must always feel like the same underlying thing — these are its rendering
laws, drawn from Book 1's practice and from how memory actually works in a body:

- **Memory arrives through the senses, strongest channel first.** Smell and taste
  outrank sound; sound outranks sight. (This is real neurology — smell is wired straight
  to emotion and memory, bypassing the brain's sensory filter; the Proust effect.) Book 1
  already obeys it: winterleaf arrives before any vision of Mara; the chair is warm *"as
  though someone had only just stood up"* before anyone is seen. When a memory must
  surface, send the smell first, the image second, the meaning last — the reader's own
  body will do the transition for you.
- **No announced flashbacks.** The trigger lives in the present scene — a smell, an
  object's weight, a phrase said in the wrong voice — and the trigger *is* the
  transition. Never open a door with *"she remembered the day when…"* and walk the
  reader through a scene-dump; the past intrudes, it is not visited. Two or three
  layered senses pin a remembered place more precisely than any paragraph of setting.
- **Each facet has its own sensory signature** — keep them distinct so the force reads
  as one thing with faces, not many devices:
  - **Inheritance** — carried objects with weight and warmth (the bird, the compass, a
    thread); what is passed *to* you arrives in the hands.
  - **Distortion** — repetition with one wrong detail; counting that stops agreeing
    with itself (forty-two bedrolls; the litany that drifts). The wrongness is stated
    flatly, never gasped at.
  - **Rediscovery** — the body knows before the mind (*"His body remembered her before
    he did"*); the knee locks on a stair it never climbed.
  - **Erasure** — absence with a shape: the unflattened grass, the name missing from a
    wall of names, *"only the shape of absence remained."* Erasure is never rendered as
    fog or blur — it is rendered as a hole with edges.
- **Memory-as-artifact is rendered flatly** (voice.md §4): recordings, anchors, the
  Archive's lights are impossible things stated plainly; dread and wonder come from
  repetition and counting, not from astonished narration.
- **Ration full immersions.** A chapter earns at most one full memory-immersion; smaller
  triggers ride inside scenes as a sentence or two. The force is everywhere; the device
  is rare — that ratio is what keeps it a force.
- **Inside a memory, the emotion laws still hold**: feelings in the body, never named;
  grief oblique; exit fast on a short line.

## Craft touchstones *(developed 2026-08)*

Reference points for register — what to take from each, and what to refuse:

- **Ursula K. Le Guin** — the plain sentence carrying wonder; the anthropologist's gaze
  (cultures observed with respect, not exoticism); *"the artist deals with what cannot
  be said in words."* Take the restraint and the humanity. Refuse nothing; she is the
  north star.
- **A Canticle for Leibowitz (Walter M. Miller Jr.)** — deep time; institutions as
  memory's carriers; the gentle irony of relics misread. Take the long view and the
  tenderness toward keepers. Refuse the despair of its cycle — MoS is
  ancient-and-optimistic.
- **Foundation (Asimov)** — succession structure, civilization as protagonist. Take the
  scale. Refuse the trade: Asimov spent characters to buy ideas; the Commandments rank
  characters above ideas.
- **Alastair Reynolds** — the sublime dark, derelicts, deep-time dread. Take the texture
  of vast cold places and the discipline that space is dangerous. Refuse the grimness as
  a destination — here, awe outranks fear.
- **Piranesi (Susanna Clarke) / Mythago Wood (Robert Holdstock)** — the numinous: the
  register Tchaikovsky names as his unclaimed goal
  ([craft-tchaikovsky](craft-tchaikovsky.md) §8) and this series' home ground. Take the
  holy calm, the labyrinth loved rather than escaped. Books 5, 8, and 22 aim here
  directly.
- **Adrian Tchaikovsky** — process, not register: world-first, information logistics,
  the one big lie. The full distillation: [craft-tchaikovsky.md](craft-tchaikovsky.md).
- **Gary Provost / Benjamin Dreyer** — the sentence mechanics layer, adopted into
  voice.md Do/Avoid: music through variation; filler cut on sight.

## Handling the uncanny, the gods, and the undefined

Approach but never answer the [intentionally undefined](../series-bible/seven-laws.md#things-that-are-intentionally-undefined).
The craft move at the boundary is *precision about the observable, silence about the
cause*: describe exactly what the characters can verify, let them disagree about what it
means, and leave. Mystery is what the characters haven't solved — never what the sentence
refuses to say plainly (the [Easy-Read Standard](commandments.md#the-easy-read-standard-the-authors-directive)).
